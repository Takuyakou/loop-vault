## Vault picker section grouping correction

Human exploratory acceptance found that one logical Vault progression appeared as
several top-level picker rows. The picker was surfacing each safe practice
section as a separate source, which made the list look duplicated.

- Candidates are now grouped by the live Vault source pair `{ ideaId, blockId }`.
  Each registered progression therefore appears once in the top-level list.
- The group preview exposes an explicit practice-section selector when several
  safe sections are available. Confirming a selection still uses its exact,
  validated snapshot; no section is silently substituted.
- The deterministic default remains useful for immediate practice: longest
  complete section first, then earliest section boundaries and signature.
- Regression coverage verifies an eight-bar source with four safe sections is
  rendered as one row, offers all four section choices, and confirms the exact
  selected snapshot. The production keyboard test was also hardened to send
  `Home` directly to its verified focused tab and assert the selected tab.

The correction does not persist Vault titles or alter Practice storage, Vault
schema, Analyzer, MIDI Exporter, audio assets, or P5.15.
