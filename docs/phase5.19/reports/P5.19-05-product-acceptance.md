## Transfer persistence hotfix

The defect was reproduced from the production build: completing Review records
History, parent persistence republishes semantically identical settings through
a new object, and the old component memoization recreated a ready session. The
completed state and Transfer control therefore disappeared. Root Motion settings
are now memoized by their scalar values, so that History publication preserves
the active session. The progress rail also shows Transfer as the final active
step. Unit and production E2E coverage exercise the complete flow through
History persistence and assert that the visible Transfer control remains active.
