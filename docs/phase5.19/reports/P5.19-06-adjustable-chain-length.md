  and legacy Practice data both default to two notes.
- Each phrase uses two beats per note, yielding deterministic four through
  sixteen-beat phrases. Level controls only the first-transition answer
  complexity.
- Listen, Sing, Play, Record & Compare target playback, fretboard disclosure,
  History, and Transfer retain the full selected sequence. Objective evidence
  remains limited to the first transition; no automatic scoring was added.
- Changing the count stops active preview playback and safely replaces the
  session. A manual Stop now remains stopped rather than advancing to Identify.
- Settings patches are merged when the serialized controller operation runs, so
  rapid Root Motion and Degree settings updates cannot overwrite one another
  from an older rendered settings object.
- Vault-derived sources fail closed when their safe root sequence is shorter
  than the selected count. They are not shortened, substituted, or represented
  as original basslines.
- The fretboard shows every frozen source/target fingering position. A repeated
  sequence step may deliberately appear at two legal physical positions; the UI
  does not invent a continuous path.
- Root Motion History supports up to seven stored motions and continues to read
  legacy entries. It keeps factual motions only, with no raw audio, source
  title/path, device data, or composite score.
