# Contract 02 — Note Evidence

Note classifier uses local texture evidence only.

Allowed evidence:

- pitch rank/topness
- lower support count/coverage
- duration contrast
- onset independence
- top-line continuity
- melodic-motion continuity
- sustained-extension protection

Forbidden:

- detected chord identity
- chord-tone membership
- winner candidate quality
- hard highest-note suppression

Raw notes are immutable.
