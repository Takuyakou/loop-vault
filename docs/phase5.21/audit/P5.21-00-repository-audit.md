## Current role inference

- Product role enum: `bass`, `harmony`, `pad`, `melody`, `percussion`, and
  `mixed` in `src/domain/midi/voiceRoles.ts`.
- The current classifier fuses measured evidence at weight `0.55`, GM-program
  evidence at `0.8`, and track-name evidence at `0.65`. Program evidence is
  not a universal hard override.
- The low-confidence fallback is `mixed` when a non-mixed top score is below
  `0.42` or its margin is below `0.08`.
- GM evidence is derived from the dominant explicit program. Program changes
  are aggregated by duration, then note count, then program number. GM 32–39
  favours bass; 112–119 favours percussion; other families are evidence only.
- Track-name hints are a small fixed list: drum/perc/kick/snare/hat, bass/sub/
  808, pad/choir/ensemble/strings, lead/solo/melody/topline/vocal, and
  chord/keys/piano/rhodes/organ/guitar.
- Channel index `9` (MIDI Channel 10) is the only hard current percussion rule.
  A manual override applies after inference except that this channel remains
  percussion.
- Current measured evidence uses max-polyphony-derived monophony, register,
  simultaneous-onset ratio, average sustained duration, density, pitch range,
  and adjacent stepwise/repeated-pitch-class ratios. It has no feature-only
  legato-repair proxy or time-weighted monophony metric.
