## Current role inference

- Product role enum: `bass`, `harmony`, `pad`, `melody`, `percussion`, and
  `mixed` in `src/domain/midi/voiceRoles.ts`.
- The current classifier fuses measured evidence at weight `0.55`, GM-program
  evidence at `0.8`, and track-name evidence at `0.65`. Program evidence is
  not a universal hard override.
- The low-confidence fallback is `mixed` when a non-mixed top score is below
  `0.42` or its margin is below `0.08`.
- GM evidence is derived from the dominant explicit program. Program changes
  are aggregated by duration, then note count, then program number. GM 32–39
  favours bass; 112–119 favours percussion; other families are evidence only.
- Track-name hints are a small fixed list: drum/perc/kick/snare/hat, bass/sub/
  808, pad/choir/ensemble/strings, lead/solo/melody/topline/vocal, and
  chord/keys/piano/rhodes/organ/guitar.
- Channel index `9` (MIDI Channel 10) is the only hard current percussion rule.
  A manual override applies after inference except that this channel remains
  percussion.
- Current measured evidence uses max-polyphony-derived monophony, register,
  simultaneous-onset ratio, average sustained duration, density, pitch range,
  and adjacent stepwise/repeated-pitch-class ratios. It has no feature-only
  legato-repair proxy or time-weighted monophony metric.

Existing UI and unit-test MIDI builders are not silently promoted to human

## Promotion gate locked before results

When the missing inputs are available, Stage 00 will measure the current
production role on the exact approved fixture registry and lock those values as
baseline. Stage 02 promotion requires all of the following:

1. Primary: manual correction burden on non-ambiguous Voices is strictly lower,
   except a zero baseline may remain zero.
2. Secondary: exact role accuracy is non-regressing; melody recall, harmony
   precision, bass precision, and percussion precision are each non-regressing;
   mixed prediction rate and the complete confusion matrix are recorded but
   cannot be the sole promotion justification.
3. Safety: official chord exact-at-1 may decline by at most `0.25` percentage
   points from the current locked baseline; root, quality, and boundary metrics
   may not regress. Scoring, boundary, and candidate-generation source diffs
   remain zero.
4. Product: human review accepts the role listing and evidence summary for the
   approved fixtures. Shadow output must be deterministic and manual override
   remains authoritative.

A missing metric, unavailable approved ground truth, or any failed condition is
`FAIL` for promotion and keeps production Role v1 unchanged. P5.21.1 mixed-
Voice note filtering is explicitly out of scope regardless of the result.
