## Harmonic Rhythm contract
Global-first candidates are 1, 2, 4, or 8 quarter-note beats, or `unknown`.
Evidence is onset/activity/PC/Bass periodicity, novelty, cell consistency/separation,
and metric alignment—not detected chord identity. Mixed, ambiguous, non-4/4, or
unsupported input fails closed to legacy. Promotion requires exact A-K results:
A-D/G-J=4, E=2, F=8, K=unknown, with K as the only fallback. Local/section estimation
remains deferred.

## Bass Lane contract
Bass State combines register, duration, strong beat, continuity, step/repetition,
texture separation, and compatible role prior. A per-attack minimum or a single Bass
PC movement is never sufficient. Walking/passing/pedal/inversion traps are protected.
Promotion requires explicit exact A-K Bass Lane results, equivalence to locked truth,
and zero unsupported stable-state violations.

## Fragment contract
PC subset/superset is only supporting evidence. Same-state evidence requires temporal,
cell, Bass, compatibility, and repetition support. Strong changes require persistent
new PC/Bass/texture evidence around a cell or metric boundary. Conflicts split or fall
back conservatively; C→Am7 and persistent C→Cmaj7 remain distinct.
