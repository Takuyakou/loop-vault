## Harmonic Rhythm results

The global estimator derives evidence from normalized upper-texture activity,
pitch-class separability, onset/metric coverage, and internally derived stable Bass
State boundaries. It does not consume fixture ids, purposes, expected lanes, chord
labels, or expected Harmonic Rhythm. The estimator is global-only and 4/4-only;
local/section estimation remains deferred.

Pedal-Bass counterexamples independently infer upper-texture periods of 1, 2, and 8
quarter beats. D/G upper changes remain visible while Bass is stable. A supported
no-change result requires at least 75% upper-texture beat coverage, non-empty first
and last beats, integer-onset evidence covering at least 25% of the complete span,
and 4/4 meter; there is no unconditional four-beat default.

## Mixed / unsupported fallback

All structural upper/Bass boundaries are combined into one complete-span segment
duration sequence. Only a uniform sequence of 1, 2, 4, or 8 is supported. Pure
3- and 6-beat cadences return `unsupported-global-periodicity`. Fixture K, and the
counterexample with one-beat alternation followed by four-beat states, return
`mixed-global-periodicity`. Non-4/4, fractional, malformed, and sparse long-span
inputs fail closed. Two isolated notes across 100,000 beats cannot obtain metric
support. K remains the only A–K fallback.

Caller-supplied Bass evidence is not trusted as an authority. The public wrapper
rederives Bass evidence from validated notes and accepts supplied evidence only when
its complete states, candidates, diagnostics, and literal operation counters are
deep-equal to that internal derivation. Fabricated `[3,3,3,3]` states, empty
candidates, or altered counters return `invalid-bass-lane` before periodicity use.

## Bass Lane results

The shadow Bass Lane exactly equals locked A–K truth and reports zero unsupported
stable/transient evidence violations. Candidate scoring combines register, duration,
strong beat, continuity, repetition/stepwise motion, actual upper-texture gap, and a
compatible role prior. A note must have at least 0.25 beat duration; independent
qualification evidence is a deep register, an actual upper-texture gap, an explicit
`bass` prior, or rooted stepwise continuity. Duration and continuity affect support
scores but are not described as independent qualification evidence. `upper` excludes it.

Lane promotion additionally requires evidence spanning at least 50% of the piece,
qualified duration coverage of at least 25%, and repeated or sustained pitch-class
support. Therefore a single 0.25-beat `bass` role prior and two widely separated
0.25-beat `bass` anchors remain unavailable. Role prior is supporting evidence only
and cannot expand sparse notes into a full-piece state.

A subsequent stable transition cannot use repetition/span alone. It additionally needs
a persistent upper boundary, repeated explicit Bass prior, or a sustained compatible
Bass motion greater than two pitch classes. Consequently:

- B keeps stable C with transient D.
- G keeps one C pedal while its upper change remains visible to Harmonic Rhythm.
- H keeps one stable C with D/E/F/G/A/B walking notes transient; MIDI 48 is accepted only through a stepwise chain rooted at or below MIDI 45.
- I preserves persistent E/G inversion Bass States without changing harmonic identity.
- Repeated passing C-C/D-D-D/C-C-C under unchanged upper harmony remains one C state with transient D.
- A 0.05-beat low outlier and repeated MIDI 47 notes marked `upper` remain unavailable.

## Fail-closed boundary

Both public estimators accept unknown runtime input and catch validation failures.
Malformed notes/meter, duplicate ids, invalid role prior, non-finite values, corrupt
caller Bass evidence, gaps/overlaps, incomplete coverage, invalid pitch classes, and
fabricated candidates/counters return unavailable/unknown without throwing. Meter,
notes, states, candidates, and transient pitch-class arrays require every own index;
sparse arrays cannot pass through skipped `every` callbacks or compare equal to dense
internally derived evidence. Candidate diagnostic fields are finite and enum/range
validated before semantic deep equality.

## Performance

Upper activity uses at most four literal event updates per upper note followed by a
fixed 12-pitch-class beat sweep. There is no per-note/per-beat overlap loop. Sorting is
`O(N log N)` and the profile pass is `O(N + 12B)`, with both notes and beats validated
at a maximum of 100,000. Run accumulation is iterative and operation counters increase
only where the named operation occurs.

The 100,000-beat held-overlap regression completes under its explicit 10,000 ms
Vitest timeout. Bass derivation reports exactly 12 profile event updates and 1,200,000
sweep cells for three held upper notes. The Harmonic Rhythm public wrapper honestly
includes both its internal Bass rederivation and its own HR pass, reporting 24 updates
and 2,400,000 sweep cells. Sparse two-note/100,000-beat input fails closed.

The locked dense benchmark used provenance `p524-dense-synthetic-E-x128-v1`, fixture E
x128, exactly 3,072 notes, three warmups, and seven measured samples. Enforcement is
an actual parent/child boundary: a parent timer terminates the benchmark child after
10,000 ms. A separate injected 25 ms timeout test delays the child by 250 ms and
observes the `timed-out` result after termination. Final watchdog-enforced run:

These are absolute Stage01 shadow timings, not same-process legacy ratios. Stage01
