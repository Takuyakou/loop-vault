## Shadow consolidator

The bounded cell core consumes the Stage01 Harmonic Rhythm and Bass Lane evidence.
For supported global rhythm it forms 1/2/4/8-quarter-beat cells; for K it uses only
the already measured persistent structural boundaries while preserving
`unknown` plus legacy fallback. It does not estimate a local/section rhythm.

Per-cell upper pitch-class activity is accumulated by binary interval lookup,
partial-edge updates, full-cell difference arrays, and a fixed 12-PC sweep. A PC
needs at least 0.5 unweighted duration-beat of activity, so A's 0.125-beat tension
does not form a stable state and J's 0.25-beat anticipation does not move the
stable boundary before beat 4.

Same-state support is local to adjacent fragments and their HR cells. A partial
subset/superset merge requires an added PC to have preceding transient Bass-lane
evidence, unweighted duration coverage `<= 0.35`, and exactly one attack. It never
uses velocity, a song-global PC set, or the current state's added PC as
self-justification. B consolidates; quiet full-cell Cmaj7 with pre-boundary passing B
and C→Am7 with passing A both remain split. A removed candidate boundary is retained
as an auditable `merge-same-state` decision with supporting evidence and reason.

Identity reuses the repository chord templates for all 12 roots by normalized PC
signature. All existing templates x12 roots resolve when stable Bass identifies the
root. Aliased signatures resolve only from a candidate-root Bass; ambiguous non-root
inversions fail closed. C6/Am7 with E Bass is explicitly unavailable. Slash display
is applied only after unambiguous identity. No A-K signature table exists.

End-exclusive lower-bound lookup clamps the final positive-overlap cell. Notes ending
at beats 4, 8, or `totalBeats` pass; zero/negative/non-finite durations fail closed.

Before Stage01 evidence or any floating accumulation, validated notes are copied into
a plain snapshot and canonically sorted by a stable total key: start, end, pitch,
velocity, role, id, then duration. Caller input is never mutated. A duration-activity
set totaling exactly the 0.5 persistence threshold produces the same exact timeline
and boundary decisions under original, reversed, rotated, and reverse-id ordering.

Strong changes retain persistent new/removed PCs, persistent Bass transition,
stable texture change, and metric-boundary evidence. PC subset/superset is recorded
as supporting evidence only and is never the sole merge rule. Consequently C→Am7
and persistent C→Cmaj7 split at beat 4, while transient tension does not flicker.
