## Local/Global arbitration
The new `localHarmonicStateShadow` module is shadow-only and is not imported by
`analysis.ts` or `harmonicStateConsolidation.ts`. It evaluates local candidates
`1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / unknown` and fails closed to legacy fallback for invalid,
unsupported, over-dense, insufficient, or valid-but-ambiguous evidence.

Candidate scores compare the observed split pattern against every real period
pattern rather than assigning only the preselected candidate a nonzero score.
Local override requires both `topScore >= 0.8` and `candidateGap >= 0.15`.
Below-threshold candidates are never labeled `local-strong`. With no internal
split, Local 4 and Local 8 remain close alternatives, so a supported Global 8
is retained for both single-bar and multi-bar inputs. A structural bar-edge
anchor lowers the continuation score for Local 8, allowing a bounded Local 4
state after a completed Local 8 pair. Exact Local 1/2 evidence can override a
Global estimate only when the threshold and margin independently pass.

All four normalized fields (`normalizedBeatStrength`, `pitchClassSupport`,
`temporalContinuity`, and `localRhythmSupport`) participate in boundary and
candidate arbitration. Per-bar diagnostics expose all candidate scores, the
top-two gap, arbitration result, and hysteresis reason. Tests cover confidence
immediately below and above threshold plus a mixed-confidence average.

## Structural Bass evidence
Persistent Bass change with stable upper structure is fail-closed whenever any
of the four normalized signals is weak: it splits instead of being accepted as
a passing merge. A persistent leap of four or more pitch classes also splits.
A small persistent arrival onto an upper-structure tone, including A to G# over
`C# E G# B`, is retained as a structural inversion and does not false-merge.

Merge retention is reason-specific. A weak non-persistent passing Bass is
excluded. A persistent same-upper Bass merge and a bar-scale inversion retain
the union of structural Bass pitch classes. When a weak first cell is followed
by persistent evidence, the weak Bass is replaced rather than becoming the
retained structural Bass. This removes the former first-cell asymmetry.

## Identity-independent consolidation
No final chord label or private note metadata enters the shadow API. Same-state
merges require independent evidence: stable upper structure, a bar-scale
same-material inversion, or same pitch material plus a bounded neighboring-bar
approach/departure. A valid bar with a non-periodic split pattern, such as
`0-1 / 1-4`, makes the entire result `unknown` with `legacyFallback: true`, even
when a supported Global estimate exists. Partial states/bars are not exposed.

## P5.26 L-Q
Fresh focused result is `PASS — 2 files / 22 tests`. L-Q canonical metadata is
evaluated as the union of upper-structure and retained Structural Bass pitch
classes; hard-fail metadata is independently asserted. L/N/P remain one state.
M/O/Q remain split; hard false merges are `0`. Passing-positive fixtures use
weak Bass persistence while hard negatives retain strong persistence, avoiding
the former maximum-confidence helper assumption. One common generator derives
all evidence from synthetic note observations only: pitch-class occupancy,
duration weighted by performed velocity, metric onset, and onset-slot coverage.
The fixture's expected decision, hard-fail flag, and known merge positions never
enter evidence generation. Passing Bass notes therefore carry lower observed
velocity while preserving their full duration and the locked production chord
snapshot; prepared synthetic data preserves that observed velocity.

New-state Structural Bass initialization uses the same minimum persistence
threshold as later consolidation. Single weak Bass and weak-to-weak sequences
retain no Structural Bass; weak-to-persistent retains only the persistent Bass,
and an already-persistent Bass remains retained.

Counterexamples cover Local 8, Global 8 single- and multi-bar retention,
valid ambiguous fallback, four-field fail-closed arbitration, small structural
inversion, merge Bass retention/asymmetry, real candidate score/gap hysteresis,
threshold boundaries, mixed confidence, directional context, canonicalization,
malformed coverage, and operation-count scaling through 4,096 bars.
