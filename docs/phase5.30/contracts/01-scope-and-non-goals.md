# Contract 01 — Scope and Non-goals

## Must ship

- Text Entry: 32 bars / 128 chord tokens, bounded and validated.
- Approved Voicing Loop compact layout.
- Fixed-geometry progression cards.
- Single-clock cyan playhead.
- Card audition without transport seek.
- `お手本音` transport checkbox.
- Automated regression/accessibility/audio-lifecycle evidence.

## Must not ship

- Practice Progressions 100 or any built-in 100-item catalog.
- Comping Pattern Library.
- Hold/Rest/Re-strike notation.
- Swing/bossa/genre groove engines.
- Arbitrary rhythmic grid, 3-chord bars, repeat symbols, or meter expansion.
- New scoring/streak/XP/mastery semantics.
- New analyzer or new voicing-generation algorithm.
- Vault schema/fileVersion migration.
- Chord Dojo/Bass Practice redesign.
- P5.19.1 dependency.
