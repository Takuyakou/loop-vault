## Protected behavior

- Text Progression remains bounded at 8,192 UTF-16 units, 32 bars and 128 chord
  tokens, with deterministic rejection beyond each limit.
- Mixed 1/2/4-chord bars preserve duration through Draft, Vault save/reload and
  all five Voicing Loop modes.
- P5.29's single clock remains the only musical-time source for current/next,
  beat, playhead, reference playback, position and loop count.
- Source MIDI and Custom keep exact pitch/octave semantics and fail closed when
  unavailable; no generated fallback was added.
- Card audition does not seek or mutate transport state. Reference sound stays
  session-local, defaults ON and releases owned audio when switched OFF.
- Pause/Resume/Restart/BPM/loop/source-switch/route-exit cleanup and rapid
  lifecycle coverage remain green.
- Keyboard-only, 320 px, effective 200%, reduced-motion and axe
  serious/critical-zero coverage remains green.

## Human acceptance checklist

- Enter and save/reload a long mixed-rhythm Text progression near the 32-bar /
  128-token boundary.
- Confirm the visual order: mode controls, Current/Next, fixed timeline,
  details, then transport.
- Let a long progression play and confirm cyan playhead timing, local
  auto-reveal, loop wrap, Pause/Resume/Restart and BPM changes by eye.
- Click and keyboard-activate non-current timeline cards; confirm the selected
  Source/Custom/Basic mode voicing sounds without moving transport position.
- Turn `お手本音` OFF during playback; confirm chord reference releases while
  visual timing and metronome continue, then confirm a new session defaults ON.
- Check real-device audio for no stuck notes, duplicate attacks or audible
  lifecycle leaks during rapid stop/restart/source switching.
- Recheck the main workspace at 320 px and effective 200% scaling with keyboard
  only and reduced motion.
