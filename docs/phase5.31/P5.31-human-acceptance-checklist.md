# P5.31 Human Acceptance Checklist

After automated P5.31-04 gates pass:

1. Open Text Progression.
2. Paste `fixtures/rechord-user-example.txt` exactly, with no manual spaces added.
3. Confirm 16 bars and the intended chord sequence.
4. Confirm bar 4 shows four 1-beat changes and ordinary fixture bars show two
   2-beat changes.
5. Save, reload, open Voicing Loop.
6. Confirm slim cards never resize; playhead moves correctly.
7. Click several progression cards and confirm audition sounds without seeking.
8. Enable `お手本音`; confirm attacks occur on chord starts/re-strikes only.
9. Paste `E 7%_A m7|=G`; confirm:
   - E7 attack,
   - E7 re-strike,
   - rest silence,
   - Am7 attack,
   - hold without re-attack,
   - G attack.
10. Confirm slash-bass display survives (`Am9/C`).
11. Test Left-hand on the exact slash families authorized by the Stage00 rule
    audit. Confirm unsupported families remain clearly unsupported.
12. Confirm duplicate unsupported messages aggregate without hiding distinct
    reasons.
13. Confirm keyboard-only use and narrow/high-scale layout are still usable.
14. Confirm no stuck notes after Pause/Restart/Stop/route exit.

Human acceptance does not authorize merge or push by itself.
