## UI review

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Voicing and display choices occupied stacked MY / LESSON sections | VOICING and DISPLAY share one compact toolbar | Keeps practice controls visible without consuming the performance surface |
| CURRENT, NEXT, and status used tall independent blocks | CURRENT / NEXT are compressed and BEAT / POSITION / LOOP share one row | Preserves the clock projection while reducing vertical travel |
| LEFT and RIGHT relied on closely related teal treatments | LEFT HAND is gold and RIGHT HAND is cyan in summaries, keyboard guides, legends, and finger labels | Makes the physical hand assignment immediately scannable without relying on color alone |
| Two score-like percentage bars duplicated transport state | The no-scoring surface keeps beat, position, loop, timeline, and playhead only | Removes a misleading mastery cue and unnecessary height |
| The keyboard and transport sat below a tall detail header | A 61-key C2-C7 keyboard fills the remaining desktop height and transport is compact | Keeps the performance reference and controls visible together |
| Timeline cards could appear to fill available width | Every progression card remains fixed at 92 by 46 pixels; long progressions scroll only inside the timeline | Preserves the established slim timeline and prevents page-level overflow |

The visible hand labels remain `LEFT HAND｜左手` and `RIGHT HAND｜右手`.
L/R finger prefixes provide a second non-color distinction.

## Layout and behavior contract

- At the tested 1920 by 1080 desktop viewport, toolbar, CURRENT, NEXT, both
  hand plans, BEAT / POSITION / LOOP, progression, 61-key keyboard, and
  transport are visible without main-content vertical or horizontal scrolling.
- Narrow layouts keep horizontal overflow local to the fixed-width progression
  timeline and the 61-key keyboard; the page itself does not overflow
  horizontally.
- Timeline cards remain audition controls only. They do not seek or replace the
  single Practice Clock.
- The playhead continues to use the existing duration-aware clock projection
  and linear interpolation, including reduced-motion handling.
- Resolved voicing, pitch/octave, fingering generation and persistence, Source
  MIDI / Custom exactness, P5.31 slash/rest/hold/re-strike semantics, audio,
  transport behavior, schemas, Analyzer, and MIDI Export are unchanged.
