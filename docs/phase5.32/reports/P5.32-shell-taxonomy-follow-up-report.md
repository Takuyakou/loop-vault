## Product taxonomy

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Three similarly named top-level Shell choices | One top-level `Shell` choice beside `Left-hand` | Presents the user's practice goal instead of implementation history |
| `Basic Full 1–7–3` | `Root Shell 1·3·7` | States the included harmonic skeleton without implying every chord tone or a fixed pitch order |
| No two-note guide-tone subtype | `Rootless Shell 3·7` | Makes 3rd/7th voice-leading an explicit, repeatable lesson |
| One undifferentiated VOICING row | Compact `MY`, `LESSON`, and conditional `SHELL TYPE` labels in the existing toolbar | Clarifies ownership and purpose without adding vertical height |

`Root Shell` remains the default whenever the user chooses `Shell`; it delegates
to the existing `basic-full` resolver. `Rootless Shell` has its own explicit
`rootless-shell` snapshot/resolver ID and places the approved 3rd/7th guide tones
in the left hand. It does not reuse or alter the four-note `Left-hand` Rootless
A/B lesson.

## Rootless rule and unsupported families

For normal seventh families the resolved degree sets are:

- minor seventh family: b3 and b7
- dominant seventh family: 3 and b7
- major seventh family: 3 and 7

The existing Lesson Rule Table remains authoritative for chord identity:

- m7b5 retains b5 in addition to b3/b7.
- altered dominant retains the approved identifying alteration, such as #5.
- 7sus4 uses 4/b7 rather than inventing a major or minor third.
- 6, 6/9, and min6 have no approved 3rd/7th Rootless Shell meaning and therefore
  return `UNSUPPORTED_RULE`.
- all other unaudited families continue to fail closed.

There is no simplification to ordinary m7/7, no Source/Custom fallback, and no
schema or file-version change.
