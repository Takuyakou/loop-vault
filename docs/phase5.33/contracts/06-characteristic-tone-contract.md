# Contract 06 — Characteristic Tones

Do not blindly apply 1/3/7 or 3/7 logic to every chord.

Protect identity tones:

- sus4: do not force 3;
- m7b5: b5 is significant;
- dim7: bb7 spelling/degree is significant;
- altered dominant: requested alterations are significant;
- 6/9: do not convert to maj9.

Unsupported is better than silent simplification.
