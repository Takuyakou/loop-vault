# Research Promotion Notes

## Highest-value findings

1. “Shell” is not a single universal rule. Production should contract on actual
   degrees/roles, not an overloaded label.
2. Source MIDI / Custom are sources, not voicing families.
3. Slash Bass must preserve the upper/root identity.
4. Literal / Reduction / Enrichment must be separate.
5. Omission is pedagogically useful data, not a hidden implementation detail.
6. Teacher-derived structures deserve provenance distinct from general theory.
7. Melody is an independent layer.
8. Candidate ranking should include loop end→start.
9. Fingering happens after voicing resolution.
10. The 33 records are a strong Golden Corpus, not a production database.

## First-wave focus

Promote what directly fixes current practice:

- teacher open structure where evidence supports it;
- guide-tone/core structures;
- Eadd9/F#;
- Dadd9/E;
- Gmaj9/A compact/full;
- Am9/C;
- Am11/B;
- C/E;
- D/C;
- m7b5;
- dim7;
- 6/9;
- sus4;
- at least one Color and one Open family.

## Do not overclaim

Most concrete octaves and fingering values in the research pack are proposals.
Human audition/playability is not yet established.

P5.33 must therefore keep rule promotion bounded and test-driven.
