## Promotion status meaning

- `PROMOTE`: the bounded harmonic rule/family is sufficiently supported for the
  Golden Corpus and later deterministic implementation; concrete register and
  fingering still remain app policy unless evidence says otherwise.
- `PROMOTE_WITH_CONSTRAINTS`: promote only the named fixture/family plus written
  context and unsupported boundary; no wider family inference.
- `DEFER`: keep as research reference, not first-wave production.
- `BLOCKED_NEEDS_HUMAN`: evidence leaves a product choice that cannot be safely
  inferred.
