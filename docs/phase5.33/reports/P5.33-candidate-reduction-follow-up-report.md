## Optimizer implementation

The deterministic bounded cyclic optimizer evaluates the complete progression,
including Last → First. Candidate-local intrinsic quality is charged at every
event so fewer notes are not rewarded merely for reducing transition distance.
Transition ranking evaluates RH/LH and inner-voice movement, guide-tone and top
continuity, common tones, register, span, note-count changes and unnecessary
leaps. Fixed melody/top remains a hard generator constraint; the optimizer only
ranks already-valid candidates.

## Practical reduction policy

- Literal/full candidates remain available when they are valid and playable.
- Teacher favors roughly four to five distinct tones; Core favors roughly
  three to four without removing identity, guide tones or the highest explicit
  extension.
- Reduction is bounded by an omission budget: normally one tone for 9th/11th
  families and up to two for 13th families. Unaltered 5 is the first practical
  omission for extended chords. Excess omissions, sparse output, excessive
  density and enrichment receive explicit intrinsic cost.
- Slash bass, major/minor/sus identity, seventh identity, altered fifths and
  explicit 9/11/13 tones are retained. Existing Core slash defaults stay
  literal, while their reduction variants remain available for comparison.

Fixture results:

- `Am11/B` keeps the formal Literal candidate (LH B; RH A, C, D, E, G) and an
  `OMIT 5` Practical Reduction candidate (LH B; RH A, C, D, G).
- `Bm11` and `Em11` expose bounded `OMIT 5` reductions while retaining 11.
- `G13` and `A13` expose bounded reductions while retaining 3, b7 and 13.
- m7b5 b5, dim7 bb7, sus4 4 and explicit extensions remain protected.
