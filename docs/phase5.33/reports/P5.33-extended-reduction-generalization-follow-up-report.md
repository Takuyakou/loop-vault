## Generalized reduction policy

Teacher and Core now derive extended-chord candidates from degree identity rather
than root names. A chord enters this path only when its represented degrees
contain a seventh identity (`7`, `b7` or `bb7`) and an extended identity (`11`,
`#11`, `13` or `b13`). The same rule therefore transposes across roots.

- m11 keeps `b3`, `b7` and `11`.
- dominant 11 keeps `3`, `b7` and `11` in the parser-supported `7(11)` / `9(11)` forms.
- dominant 13 keeps `3`, `b7` and `13`.
- m13 keeps `b3`, `b7` and `13`.
- maj13 keeps `3`, `7` and `13`.
- Slash bass stays fixed and the upper structure remains rooted in X for X/Y.

The Candidate Set retains a playable Literal form, adds `OMIT 5`, and adds
`OMIT 5 · 9` when the natural 9 is implied rather than explicitly requested.
The unaltered perfect fifth remains the first omission. The omission budget is
two only for the degree-identified 11th/13th reduction family; unrelated chord
families keep their existing budget.

An explicit natural 9 and explicit `b9`, `#9`, `#11` and `b13` are never removed
by this reduction. A three-note chromatic run that cannot satisfy the physical
right-hand guard is resolved by a degree-based hand/register redistribution:
the guide tone and seventh anchor exchange hands. No semitone tone is silently
deleted, slash bass is never moved, and all resulting placements still pass the
existing span, register, hand-crossing and low-interval guards.

## Candidate quality and optimizer

Every candidate retains honest coverage:

- no omissions: `Literal`
- one or more omissions: `Performance Reduction`
- modifier-added material: `Creative Enrichment`

Teacher omission cost increases from the single-fifth reduction to the stronger
fifth-plus-ninth reduction. Sparse, excessive-omission and density penalties
remain candidate-intrinsic, so the progression optimizer cannot select a thin
voicing merely because it minimizes motion. Optimization ON/OFF is tested for
both Teacher and Core; selected plans retain 11th/13th identity and all explicit
alterations.

## Representative fixture results

- m11 roots `Am11`, `Bm11`, `Cm11`, `F#m11`: Literal, `OMIT 5`, and
  `OMIT 5 · 9` candidates; `b3`, `b7`, `11` retained.
- dominant 13 roots `C13`, `D13`, `G13`, `A13`, `Bb13`: the same three
  candidate classes; `3`, `b7`, `13` retained.
- `Cm13` and `Cmaj13`: parser-supported aliases preserve their respective
  minor/major third, seventh and natural 13 identities.
- dominant 11: `C7(11)` and `C9(11)` produce degree-based reductions.
  `C7(11,b9)` and `C7(11,#9)` retain both 11 and the explicit altered ninth.
- altered 13: `C13(b9)`, `D13(#9)`, `G13(b13)`, `A13(#11)` retain the
  explicit alteration in every generated candidate.
- `Am11/B`: slash B remains the hard bass reference; upper degrees stay on the
  A-minor basis and the reduction does not duplicate or reroot the slash bass.
- m7b5 b5, dim7 bb7, sus4 4, 6/9 identities and add9-without-seventh remain
  covered by regression tests.

The existing domain has no standalone dominant-11 quality. The supported and
tested representation is `7(11)` / `9(11)`; no schema/parser-family expansion
was introduced. The `M13` versus `m13` alias collision discovered by these tests
was corrected so uppercase `M13` remains major and lowercase `m13` remains minor.
