## Cause and correction

The runtime resolver treated promoted research records as an exact chord
whitelist. A missing promoted record therefore made an otherwise ordinary
chord unsupported. The follow-up replaces that runtime lookup with a
deterministic chord-family generator. The research JSON and S01-S16 remain
non-runtime semantic validation evidence.

- Teacher, Core, Color and Open are independent generation strategies.
- Teacher output records `teacher-derived-generalized`; generated pitches,
  octaves and fingering are not represented as teacher-specified facts.
- Slash chords retain `X/Y`, use X as the degree basis, keep Y as the separate
  bass, and never reroot the upper harmony.
- Color additions are reported as `creative-enrichment`.
- Open keeps slash bass fixed and prioritizes wider upper-voice placements.
- Source MIDI and Custom continue to use exact saved pitches and octaves.
- Existing cyclic voice-leading, fingering, clock, audition and playback paths
  remain in use.
