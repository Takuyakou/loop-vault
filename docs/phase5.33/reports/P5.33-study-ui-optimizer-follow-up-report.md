## Cause and correction

The Study toolbar represented Teacher, Core, Color and Open as four mutually
exclusive choices even though Color and Open are modifiers. Progression
selection always used cyclic ranking, so there was no honest chord-local OFF
state. Card audition highlighted a card but did not update CURRENT, NEXT or the
keyboard. MIDI status shared the already-dense primary transport row. Octave
displacement was unavailable.

The reported top F-sharp case also exposed an audition boundary risk:
`releaseAll` and the next polyphonic attack were scheduled at the same audio
instant. Depending on the audio backend, that release could affect a newly
allocated voice, commonly the top voice.

The follow-up makes these corrections:

- Teacher/Core are the exclusive base Study strategies.
- Color and Open are independent, composable modifiers.
- `進行に合わせて最適化` selects bounded deterministic cyclic ranking;
  OFF selects each chord's deterministic local default.
- Source MIDI and Custom remain exact and disable the lesson-only controls.
- An OCT -/+ control beside Key applies a session-only -2..+2 octave
  displacement without mutating saved/source snapshots.
- Clicking a progression card updates CURRENT, NEXT and the 88-key display,
  while transport position, playhead, `aria-current` and Resume remain at the
  original clock position.
- MIDI status, reconnect, settings and session status occupy a compact second
  transport row.
- Card audition separates release and full-polyphonic attack by 12 ms. The
  Gmaj9/A regression passes A2, F#3, B3, D4 and F#4 unchanged to the instrument.
- The optimizer adds explicit top-voice motion cost while retaining the
  last-to-first cyclic edge.

The UI follows the existing Loop Vault tokens and compact practice-surface
language. No new animation or navigation system was introduced.

## Safety and semantics

- The formal eight-chord fixture resolves 8/8 in all eight
  Teacher/Core × Color OFF/ON × Open OFF/ON combinations.
- Color OFF adds no creative tones; Color ON records enrichment explicitly.
- Open keeps slash bass fixed while widening upper voices.
- A fixed melody top remains a hard constraint.
- 128-event generation remains bounded and deterministic.
- Source MIDI/Custom exactness and generated fallback boundaries remain
  explicit.
- Octave changes are session-only and fail closed outside MIDI 0..127.
- Vault schema, fileVersion, clock, saved data and source MIDI are unchanged.
