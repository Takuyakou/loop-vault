## 3. Candidate-pipeline detail

- **S01 (Abmaj9)** — generated and top-1 (`Abmaj9@1.189`); all source pcs
  explained, none invented. Correct.
- **S02 (G7(#9,b13,no5))** — not exactly representable ("no5" unsupported). The
  top-1 is `Ebadd9/G`: it explains {Eb,F,G,Bb} but **leaves the defining major
  3rd (B, pc 11) unexplained** and reinterprets the b13 (Eb) as root. Candidate
  family is Eb-dominated (Ebadd9/G, Eb6/9/G, Eb9/G, …); the forbidden Fm11/G is
  rank 6. This is a representability limit that surfaces as a wrong family, not
  a ranking failure.
- **S03 (Bm7)** — correct.
- **S04 (B11(no5))** — not representable. Top-1 `B9` explains {C#,D#,A,B}, but
  **invents F# (5th) and leaves the 11 (E, pc 4) unexplained**. The major 3rd
  (D#, pc 3) is preserved (no minor-family erasure). `Bm11` is present but not
  top-1. Representability limit.
- **S05 (C/E)** — `C/E` generated and top-1 (`C/E@1.110`, structural bass match
  true); correct. The inversion/slash path works here.
- **S06 (G7sus4)** — correct (sus4 preserved, no forced major 3rd).
- **S07 (Bm7b5)** — correct (b5 preserved, not normalized away).
