## Recommended next phase: Contextual identity / candidate ranking

LF-MIDI-001's dominant remaining failure is **contextual slash-chord mislabeling**:
a correct narrow chord (e.g. Bm7) is generated but loses to a broad slash chord
(Am11/B) when a 2-beat window is contaminated by sustained/overlapping notes
from adjacent harmonies.

Recommended focus (diagnostic first, then a bounded, gate-protected change):

1. **Isolate the ranking vs segmentation contribution.** Use the P5.34 candidate
   diagnostics to measure, per contaminated window, how much of the loss is
   (a) broad-template over-coverage vs (b) carried-over note weight. This keeps
   the fix targeted.
2. **Candidate ranking / over-coverage penalty.** Prefer the narrowest chord
   that explains the salient (highest-weight) pitch classes; penalize
   candidate-only pitch classes and slash-bass reinterpretation when the bass is
   better explained as the root.
3. **Note weighting for carryover.** Down-weight sustained notes whose onset is
   outside the window (active-note carryover) rather than treating overlap
   duration as full evidence.

Success gate: the corrected S01–S07 corpus (S03 Bm7 stays top-1; S06/S07 sus4 /
m7b5 characteristics preserved; S02/S04 remain representability limits and are
not silently "fixed") plus a new contamination fixture that reproduces the
Am11/B-vs-Bm7 pattern.

## Secondary: bounded meter display/label handling

The meter-driven bar/block/text fragmentation is CONFIRMED but is a
display/structure problem, not a harmonic problem. A bounded phase would align
the formatted bar/block structure with the stable harmonic timeline (e.g. derive
bar labels from harmonic boundaries rather than the raw 1/4 meter). It must not
change harmonic identity and must not be a global 1/4→4/4 rewrite.
