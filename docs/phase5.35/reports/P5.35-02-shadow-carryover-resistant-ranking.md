## What it does

For each production window it builds a **shadow histogram** identical to the
production histogram except that contributions the Stage-01 classifier labels
`CARRIED_IN_SUSTAIN` are multiplied by an attenuation factor `α`, then re-ranks
the **same candidate set** with the **same scorer** and reports legacy top-1 vs
shadow top-1. Nothing is wired into the analyzer.

## Same candidate set, same scorer (fidelity)

The candidate set is production's fixed 12 roots × chord-quality templates; it is
independent of the histogram. The shadow histogram is scored by the **exact
exported production scorer** through `matchWindow(...)`, so the *only* variable
between legacy and shadow is the histogram. The per-note weight is a mirror of
`buildWeightedWindows` (`overlapBeats × beatPositionFactor × rangeFactor ×
velocityFactor × roleFactor × simultaneityBonus`, bass histogram ×1.25).

**Mirror guard:** the test `attenuation=1 reproduces the production histogram
bit-for-bit` asserts, for every window of several fixtures, that the mirrored
histogram `toEqual` production's `buildWeightedWindows(...).histogram`. If the
production weight formula ever drifts, this fails loudly. At `α=1` the shadow
top-1 equals the legacy top-1 in every window of every fixture (no-op control).

## Anti-oracle & candidate-dependent evidence

Roles are derived only from runtime observations — onsets, durations, attack /
sustain sets, the freshly-attacked bass, and a stable-tone chord vocabulary. **No
fixture chord label enters** the classifier or the scorer. Attenuation acts on a
note's contribution to the shared candidate histogram (candidate-dependent
evidence), never on any label.

## Carryover-resistance demonstration (the payoff)

`contamination` fixture (prior C/E/G sustain briefly into a window where Bm7 —
low B, D, F#, A — attacks), `α = 0`:

| window | legacy top-1 | shadow top-1 | carried pcs | changed |
|--------|--------------|--------------|-------------|---------|
| w1 (bar 1) | `Bm11` (1.00) | `Bm7` (1.00) | C, E, G (0,4,7) | **yes** |
| others | `C` / empty | identical | — | no |

The contaminated histogram let the **broader** `Bm11` over-explain the window —
its 9th/11th (C#/E) and other tones were partly supplied by the carried C/E/G.
Attenuating the carryover recovers the **correct narrower** `Bm7`. This is
exactly P5.34's second failure family ("context-contaminated evidence → a broad
candidate beats the correct narrower one"), resolved at the ranking layer in
shadow — with the structural bass (B) still correctly the bass.

## Legitimate-harmony safety (no regression)

At full attenuation (`α = 0`) the shadow top-1 is **unchanged** and
`carriedContributionCount = 0` for every one of:

- held **maj9** (rich harmony) — `Cmaj9` stays `Cmaj9` (9/11/13 not penalized);
- **pad / long sustain** — held, unchanged;
- **re-articulated common tone** (Cmaj7 → Am9, shared G re-struck);
- **unrearticulated common tone** (Cmaj → Am, shared E sustained, not re-struck);
- **C/E slash** — unchanged (bass E is a flag, not attenuated).

By construction: held windows produce no carryover, re-/un-articulated common
tones are protected as `COMMON_TONE`, and current attacks/short current tones are
never attenuated. Safety is a property of the Stage-01 role model, not a tuned
exception.

## Ablation / sensitivity (§5)

- Changed-window count over `α ∈ {1, 0.5, 0}` on `contamination`: `α=1 → 0`
  (control), attenuation only ever adds changes (`count(α=0) ≥ count(α=1)`).
- `competingNewHarmony ≥ 2` sensitivity — `guideToneChange` (only two guide tones
  attack over a sustained foreign bass): the 2-tone change meets the threshold, so
  the foreign sustained tone is attenuable; at `α=0` the window moves
  `G13/E → Bdim`. Reported as a **diagnostic** of the threshold's reach on sparse
  changes, not a claimed correct label; the threshold is unchanged and remains a
  heuristic for later tuning under the promotion gates. It was **not** loosened to
  improve any fixture.

## Determinism & boundedness

set per window inside `matchWindow`. `data` is never mutated (asserted).
