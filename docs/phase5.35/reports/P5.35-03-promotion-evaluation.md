### Q1 — Does the known context-contaminated failure improve?

**No, not materially.** Only 1 of 33 windows changes, and it is **not** one of the
known wrong-root failure windows. The whole 65-bar file contains a single
`CARRIED_IN_SUSTAIN` contribution, so there is almost no carryover for the policy
to attenuate.

### Q2 — Does the correct candidate rise as carryover over-support is reduced?

**Not demonstrated.** In the failure windows the correct candidate does not rise —
`Am11/B` / `Fm11/G` persist. Those broad wrong-root labels are supported by
**currently-attacked** evidence (re-articulated each 1/4-beat), not by carryover,
so carryover attenuation does not reach them.

### Q3 — Is the structural bass preserved?

**Yes.** The bass interpretation is unchanged everywhere (the single altered window
keeps bass Bb; all slash windows keep their bass root under shadow). P5.35 did not
disturb bass detection.

### Q4 — Wrong-root slash (`Am11/B`-type): removed / weakened / unchanged?

**Unchanged.** `Am11/B` and `Fm11/G` appear identically in legacy and shadow. The
shadow method neither removes nor weakens them.

### Q5 — New regression elsewhere in the same MIDI?

No widespread regression — 32/33 windows are identical to legacy. The single change
(`Bbm11 → Eb13/Bb`) is in a non-failure window and cannot be confirmed as an
improvement (no oracle); it is a lateral extended-chord move, not a recovery.

## Why Gate A fails (root cause)

LF-MIDI-001's contextual failures are the wrong-root minor-slash mislabels
(`Am11/B`, `Fm11/G`). Under production's 1/4-meter windowing their defining tones
are **re-articulated** (attacked) every beat, so the classifier labels them
`CURRENT_ATTACK`, not `CARRIED_IN_SUSTAIN`. The broad candidate over-explains the
window from **currently-attacked** evidence, not from sustained carryover.
Carryover attenuation — the entire mechanism of the P5.35-02 shadow policy — has
nothing to attenuate there. The dominant distortion is meter fragmentation
(P5.34 family 1), which P5.35 explicitly does not address (non-goal). The phase's
working hypothesis ("the private failure is carryover contamination") is **not
supported** for LF-MIDI-001 under production windowing.
