## What P5.35 established (kept findings)

1. The carryover-resistant shadow model is **technically valid and
   regression-safe** on the tested synthetic corpus: it corrects the synthetic
   contaminated case `Bm11 → Bm7` while leaving held/pad/legato, re-articulated and
   unrearticulated common tones, rich extended harmony (Cmaj9/Abmaj9), C/E slash,
   and boundary/arpeggio/re-strike **unchanged** (0 legitimate-harmony regression).
2. It is **not relevant enough to the real LF-MIDI-001 failure to promote.**
   LF-MIDI-001 contained overwhelmingly current-attack evidence
   (**577 CURRENT_ATTACK / 1 CARRIED_IN_SUSTAIN** across 65 bars / 33 windows), so
   the mechanism changed only 1 of 33 windows and left the real wrong-root slash
   mislabels (`Am11/B`, `Fm11/G`) **unchanged**.

## Causal interpretation update (careful)

- The real wrong-root slash failure (`Am11/B`, `Fm11/G`) is **not** primarily
  caused by carried-in sustain. Under production's 1/4-meter windowing the tones
  that drive those labels are re-articulated (attacked), i.e. current-attack
  evidence — not sustained carryover.
- The useful P5.35 finding is preserved as-is: **carryover attenuation can correct
  the synthetic contaminated `Bm11 → Bm7` case, but this mechanism does not
  materially correct the real `Am11/B` / `Fm11/G` failures.**
- P5.34's observation of an **over-dense / context-contaminated fixed window may
  still be valid**, but P5.35 shows the real contamination in LF-MIDI-001 is **not
  primarily sustained carryover**.
- A **leading next hypothesis (NOT confirmed)**: multiple *current* attacks are
  being grouped into the same fixed analysis window under the 1/4-meter topology,
  so a single window contains more than one chord's freshly-attacked tones and a
  broad candidate over-explains the merged set. This is a hypothesis for a future
  phase; **it is not confirmed here**, and no work in P5.35 tests or implements it.
