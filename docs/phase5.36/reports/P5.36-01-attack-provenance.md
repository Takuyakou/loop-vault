## Diagnostic seam

`analyzeAttackProvenance(data, roles, options)` (`scripts/p536/attackProvenance.ts`)
reads the same evidence notes production uses (`selectChordEvidenceNotes`, minus
percussion), reproduces the fixed legacy 2-beat window grid, and splits each
window into `bucketCount` equal beat buckets (default 2 × 1 beat: B0 = [start,
start+1), B1 = [start+1, start+2)). It is pure, deterministic (exact-onset
clusters, fixed order, no clock/random), bounded (notes × windows), and never
mutates `data`. It calls no production ranking; candidate rank/score/histogram are
untouched.

## Attack-provenance model

Per attacked contribution: `windowIndex`, `windowStart/EndBeat`,
`absoluteAttackBeat`, `relativeAttackBeat`, `beatBucket`, `pitchClass`,
`voiceRole`, `structuralBass` flag, `durationOverlapBeats`, exact-onset
`onsetClusterId`, `attackOrderInWindow`. Per bucket: attack count, unique PCs,
structural-bass-attack count, onset-cluster ids, duration-overlap, and the
`bassPc` = lowest bass-ish note *sounding* in the bucket (incl. sustained, so a
held bass is not read as changing). Per window: `unionPcCount`,
`maxSingleBucketPcCount`, `pcInflation = union − maxSingleBucket`,
`crossBucketOverlapPcCount`, `crossBucketJaccard`, `bassChange`,
`onsetClusterCount`.

**Critical rule held (§3):** the instrument records where attacks land in time; it
**never** decides how many harmonies a window contains. `CURRENT_ATTACK` is not
equated with `SAME_HARMONIC_STATE_ATTACK`, and `>1 attack region` is never treated
as `>1 harmony`.

### Hard negatives (the P5.37 over-split guards)

- **Same-chord re-attack** and **arpeggio/rolled** show `>1` onset cluster but the
  union is still one chord's PC set; re-attack keeps `pcInflation = 0`.
- **Layered extended chord** shows `pcInflation = 2` and disjoint buckets yet a
  **held bass** and a single valid Cmaj9 — proving that inflation + bucket
  disjointness **alone do not imply two harmonies**. This is the key negative that
  P5.37 must not over-split.
- **Structural-bass change** shows `bassChange = true` with `pcInflation = 0` —
  proving a bass move alone (P5.34/P5.35: bass ≠ root truth) **is not** a harmony
  change.

So no single metric is decisive; the wrong-root profile below is a *combination*.

## PC inflation (§10)

`pcInflation = unionPcCount − maxSingleBucketPcCount`. Clean windows: **0** in all
four (both buckets carry the *same* Abmaj9 PC set). Wrong-root windows: **2–4**
(union 7–10 spread over buckets of only 4–6 PCs). The merged 2-beat histogram
therefore spans **more pitch classes than either beat actually contains** — exactly
the widening that lets a broad/wrong-root slash candidate over-explain the window.

## Cross-bucket similarity (§11)

Clean windows: Jaccard **1.00** (buckets identical → Case A, one sustained/re-struck
harmony). Wrong-root windows: Jaccard **0.00** (REAL-WR-02/03) or **0.43**
(REAL-WR-01/04) → Case B, the two beats carry materially different pitch evidence.
REAL-WR-02/03 are fully disjoint; REAL-WR-01/04 are partial. Stage 01 does **not**
conclude a harmony count from this.

## Structural-bass provenance (§16)

All four wrong-root windows show a **bass change between buckets** (REAL-WR-02/03:
C→B; REAL-WR-01/04: G#→G), while all four clean controls hold a constant bass
(Ab). Notably, for `Am11/B` the slash bass **B comes from bucket 1**, while bucket
0 sounds a **C** bass — the merged window blends a C-bass region and a B-bass
region, and `bassPc = maxIndex(bassHistogram)` then reports B while the pitch
content still includes bucket-0's C. This is consistent with the `Am11/B` mislabel
being an artefact of merging two bass regions. Per P5.34/P5.35, a bass change alone
is **not** asserted to be a harmony change (the `structural-bass change` hard
negative shows a bass move with 0 inflation).

## Answers (Q1–Q7)

- **Q1 — density higher in wrong-root?** **No.** Clean controls average **16.8**
  contributions vs wrong-root **12.8**. Raw density is **not** the discriminator;
  the P5.36-00 "12–14" figure is not specific to wrong-root windows.
- **Q2 — different PC evidence in the two buckets?** **Yes.** Wrong-root Jaccard
  0.00–0.43 and cross-bucket overlap 0–3, vs clean Jaccard 1.00 / overlap 5.
- **Q3 — union PC count inflated beyond a single bucket?** **Yes.** Wrong-root
  inflation 2–4 (union 7–10 over 4–6-PC buckets); clean inflation 0.
- **Q4 — does same-chord re-attack inflate similarly?** **No.** The synthetic
  same-chord re-attack and the clean re-struck/held `Abmaj9` windows both have
  `pcInflation = 0`. Re-attack does not inflate; inflation separates re-attack from
  genuinely different per-beat evidence.
- **Q5 — difference from a layered extended chord?** A layered extended chord
  (Cmaj9) also inflates (2) with disjoint buckets, but keeps a **held bass**
  (`bassChange = false`) and forms one tertian stack. The wrong-root windows add a
  **bass change** (4/4) on top of inflation + low similarity. Inflation alone is
  therefore **not** sufficient — the layered fixture is the explicit over-split
  guard for P5.37.
- **Q6 — structural-bass change in wrong-root windows?** **Yes, in all four**
  (C→B, G#→G), vs 0/4 clean.
- **Q7 — leading hypothesis verdict:** **`SUPPORTED-HYPOTHESIS`.** Wrong-root
  windows consistently merge two beat-buckets that carry distinct pitch evidence
  (inflation 2–4, Jaccard ≤0.43) and a changing bass, whereas clean windows do not
  (inflation 0, Jaccard 1.0). This is not raised to `CONFIRMED`: Stage 01 does not
  determine harmony, and it has not yet shown that partitioning restores the
  intended candidate rank — that is the job of P5.36-02 (meter-normalized identity
  A/B) and P5.36-03 (sub-window score decomposition).

## Causal classification

`SUPPORTED-HYPOTHESIS` for: "multiple current attacks from distinct beat regions →
one over-dense 2-beat histogram whose widened PC + blended bass support a
wrong-root/broad-slash candidate." The refinement vs P5.36-00 is important: the
signal is **PC inflation + cross-bucket divergence + bass change**, **not** raw
contribution density.

## Remaining uncertainties

- Whether re-partitioning the histogram at the beat/bucket level actually restores
  the intended narrower candidate rank (P5.36-03).
- Whether a diagnostic 4/4 meter view changes the top-1 identity on these windows
  (P5.36-02).
- REAL-WR-01/04 are only partially divergent (Jaccard 0.43, semitone bass move
  G#→G); they are weaker instances than REAL-WR-02/03 (disjoint, C→B) and must not
  be over-read.
- The layered-extended-chord and structural-bass-change hard negatives bound any
  future split rule so P5.37 does not fragment legitimate extended harmony or
  slash chords.
