## CONFIRMED families

### Family A — meter-derived downstream fragmentation — `CONFIRMED`

`1/4 meter → beatsPerBar = 1 → bar/block/formatted-text topology fragmentation`.
Evidence: P5.36-02 diagnostic 4/4 view collapses `totalBars 65 → 17` (fewer
bars/blocks) while leaving chord identity untouched. This is a formatting/structure
effect, not a harmonic-identity effect.

### Family B — fixed 2-beat evidence mixing → wrong-root identity — `CONFIRMED`

`fixed 2-beat window → distinct beat-level attack states unioned → one histogram →
evidence from different temporal states read as one simultaneous harmony →
chimera/broad/wrong-root slash candidate wins`. Evidence (P5.36-03, LF-MIDI-001, 4
targets, parity 98/98):

- union-only winner **4/4** (W2 winner wins in neither beat),
- wrong **root** wins neither beat **4/4** (a pure union artifact),
- winner support spans both beats **4/4** (chimera),
- one beat restores a coherent chord — `Am11/B` → **B1 alone = `Bm7`** (root = the
  slash bass = Case B).

Synthetic true-two-harmony reproduces it (`A7` wins neither beat); all hard
negatives are correctly not flagged.
