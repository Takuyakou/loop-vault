## Anonymous review protocol

The two changed private regions are assigned `FC-REAL-01` and `FC-REAL-02`
in source-time order. Selection uses only the already frozen legacy/Shadow
identity difference. Selection is distinct from adjudication: a changed label
is not evidence that either label is musically correct.

Each ignored-local packet contains a short source-note excerpt, one beat of
context on either side where available, note onsets and durations, low-note
motion, and pitch classes relative to the lowest target pitch class. The
source-only page contains no analyzer label, Shadow label, score, penalty,
rank, or desired answer. A separate candidate page offers two interpretations
as A/B without identifying their origin. The A/B origin map remains local and
sealed until the reviewer responds.

The reviewer considered source evidence and made a choice from A, B, both
plausible, neither, or insufficient evidence before the A/B origin map was
unsealed. The final classification for each ID is one of `CONFIRMED-FAMILY-C`,
`CONFIRMED-LEGACY`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or `INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE`. No candidate
score or expected Family-C win may influence that classification.
