# P5.40-01 — Shadow candidate-generation design lock

This rule is frozen before Stage01 implementation. It addresses the Stage00
generalizable gap between the bounded semantic catalog and the frozen 276-row
ranking enumeration. It is a Shadow-only reachability experiment, not a
Promotion claim or a change to production.

## Exact rule

Keep the first 276 Stage02 candidates in exactly their current order. Inspect
the already-bounded, individually supported dominant-alteration neighbor
archetypes in root-major and catalog order. For an archetype/root pair, generate
one additional candidate only when the weighted source pitch-class histogram
materially contains its root, major third, minor seventh, and named alteration
using the scorer's existing presence threshold. The natural fifth must also
be materially present because these fixed neighbors contain a fifth; this
protects the existing no5 targets from being treated as with-fifth evidence.
The observed structural bass
must belong to that candidate's upper pitch-class template. Attach that bass
as an independent slash fact only when it differs from the root. Require a
valid, unique bounded semantic identity.

If more than 24 additional candidates qualify, generate **none** of them for
that window. Otherwise append all qualifying candidates in the fixed order.
No arbitrary top-24 selection, modifier powerset, inferred omissions, or
answer lookup is permitted. Candidate visits are therefore 276–300/window.

## Frozen scoring and isolation

The additional candidates use the existing Shadow score formula, quality
evidence, explicit-modifier penalty, omission handling, and score/label/index
tie-break. No coefficient, threshold, penalty amount, tie-break, Family B,
smoothing, or production path changes. The old Stage02 ranker remains an exact
276-candidate control; the new Stage01 entrypoint opts into the bounded
generation rule. Disabling that entrypoint is the rollback condition.

## Counterfactual and controls

Intended change: a structurally supported, individually named alteration and
compatible observed bass become reachable before ranking, across all roots.
Not intended to change: ordinary major, minor, dominant, suspended, slash,
omission-sensitive, sparse, or unsupported-modifier evidence; existing Stage02
outputs; source notes/timing/order; Family A/B and smoothing behavior.

Synthetic all-root positive and negative controls, order/register/octave/
doubling metamorphics, dense over-bound fail-closed input, repeated determinism,
source immutability, exact old-scorer parity, and private aggregate reachability
must be checked. A generated candidate need not rank first; ranking safety and
whole-file Promotion are deferred to separately authorized later stages.

## Privacy and stop

Only anonymous aggregates may appear in tracked reports. Source-derived
identities, note content, ranks, and numerical comparisons remain ignored-local.
Stop after Stage01 focused Shadow validation. Do not begin Stage02 or perform
private Promotion evaluation, production integration, merge, or push.
