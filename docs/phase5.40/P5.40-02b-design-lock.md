# P5.40-02b — Final Shadow Correction Design Lock

Frozen before correction-code changes. This is one final Shadow-only attempt,
not an authorization to retune after observing the final private evaluation.
The independently frozen human classifications and historical FAIL decisions
are immutable inputs. Source-specific identities, notes, positions, ranks and
scores remain ignored-local.

## Causal basis

Stage02a isolated one bounded-grammar mismatch and one exact-score tie loss.
The first divergence of both protected end-to-end failures is ranking; changed
ranking inputs toggle the existing Family B decision, while smoothing is not
the first cause. The newly reviewed region is independently classified as a
temporal mixture. Both prior Shadow projections failed to separate it.

Ranking/grammar alone cannot represent every such temporal change: the frozen
Family B predicate requires different local roots, whereas a coherent
time-varying harmony can retain its root. Its one-beat buckets also cannot
locate a change inside a beat. This is a structural limitation of the existing
projection, independent of which correct same-root candidate wins. Therefore
an additional **Shadow-only** source-evidence partition probe is justified;
the production Family B helper and production smoother stay unchanged.

## One fixed correction

1. Preserve the frozen 276-candidate prefix and Stage01 bounded individual
   alterations. Add only two fixed, root-transposed semantic upper templates:
   a minor-seventh structure naming both ninth and eleventh, and an
   omitted-fifth dominant structure naming the fixed root-relative +6 and
   +8 upper alterations. Generate an entry only when every structural and named
   tone is material in that window, its omitted fifth is not material when
   applicable, and the observed bass belongs to the upper template. Attach
   a compatible slash bass independently. The complete addition set is
   bounded at 24; if it exceeds that limit, fail closed to the frozen 276.
   No arbitrary top-K truncation, modifier powerset or private lookup.
2. Keep all existing numeric scores and penalties. On an **exact** score tie
   between candidates with the same root, bass and pitch-class template,
   resolve only by semantic evidence: first prefer fewer contradictions
   between a named altered structural fifth and a materially present natural
   fifth; then prefer more materially present, explicitly named extensions.
   If neither distinguishes them, retain the prior label/index tie order.
   This must not promote unsupported modifiers or alter non-tied scores.
3. Rank W2 and local buckets with that Shadow policy, then call the existing
   Family B helper unchanged. For this unpromoted Shadow experiment, a changed
   Family B trigger is a safety boundary: retain the frozen 276-path partition
   decision. If that decision is unsplit, retain its W2 winner; if split, use
   the new local winners within the frozen partition. This conservative guard
   prevents newly generated ranking winners from toggling a previously
   confirmed partition before a separate Promotion decision. It is applied
   uniformly to every window, never by anonymous ID.
4. Only where the guarded path remains unsplit, probe four source-derived
   half-beat windows per W2. Weight an overlapping note by its exact overlap
   duration and velocity; take structural bass from the lowest materially
   overlapping note. A proposed half-beat state requires at least three
   materially present pitch classes, candidate hit ratio >= 0.85, outside
   ratio <= 0.15 and no missing expected tone. Split at a half-beat boundary
   only when an actual note onset occurs there, at least one formerly active
   pitch class ends, the neighboring upper templates have at least two
   differing pitch classes, and both neighboring states meet those support
   thresholds. Bass-only changes, unchanged upper templates, sparse bins and
   same-state re-strikes therefore abstain. Rank each proposed local state
   with the same bounded policy. Feed the resulting
   spans to the **unchanged** production smoother. If the evidence is sparse
   or ambiguous, abstain and retain the guarded coarse span. No MIDI rewrite.

The ordering is grammar/generation → semantic tie resolution → frozen Family B
helper and partition-stability guard → optional source-evidence micro-partition
→ unchanged smoothing → final timeline. The guard and micro-partition are
Shadow-only evaluation policy; no `src/**` or default analyzer change.

## Counterfactuals and protected scope

Intended: exact semantic reachability, evidence-resolved ties, restoration of
previously confirmed and necessary temporal decisions, and protection of the
new independent temporal classification. Not intended: changes to ordinary
major/minor/dominant/suspended/slash controls, source truth, Family A,
production Family B, default analysis, parser, schema, persistence or export.
Test all roots, source order/register/octave/doubling invariants where
musically appropriate, omission and alteration negatives, same-state
re-attacks, bass-only changes, sustained notes, and sparse half-beat evidence.

## Bound and final decision

Every W2, one-beat and optional half-beat rank call must visit at most 300
candidates. The final private whole-file evaluation is performed once after
synthetic/protected tests. Any new unreviewed change, unresolved reviewed
divergence, lost required split, failed protected region, non-determinism,
source mutation or bound breach means `SHADOW SAFETY = FAIL`. No post-result
retune or second correction in this stage. PASS would authorize only a
separate Stage03 decision, not production. STOP after PASS or FAIL.
