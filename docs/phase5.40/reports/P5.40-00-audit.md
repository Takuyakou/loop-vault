## Diagnosis and decision

**DIAGNOSIS = GENERALIZABLE-CAUSE-IDENTIFIED. P5.40-00 = PASS.** This is a
Stage00 research outcome, not a policy-correction or Promotion verdict. The
identified mechanism is a gap between semantic identity support and the frozen
candidate enumeration, with the resulting ranking limited to alternatives
that were generated. It is not justified to explain an absent correct
candidate by a fabricated score margin. Synthetic controls across all 12
roots reproduced the mechanism without private-ID or source-position rules.
The anti-overfit audit found no case-specific scoring or candidate branch;
private IDs are evaluation locators only. The next stage, if separately
authorized, must investigate a general correction and its safety rather than
retuning this private case. Stage01 has not started.
