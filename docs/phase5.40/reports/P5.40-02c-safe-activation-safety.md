## Activation failure classification

The stored private trace identifies two general mechanisms without exposing
identities or positions:

1. **Over-broad identity activation.** The structural/conflict checks
   certified 46 evaluated bucket winners and rejected two on conflict. The
   final timeline still changed in eight unreviewed regions; changed-region
   classification is predominantly identity selection (9 of 10). Material
   support and a strict semantic difference alone did not limit activation
   to safe whole-file scope.
2. **Temporal direction and coverage.** In the required-split case, both
   local winners passed identity certification, but a baseline split versus
   proposed non-split passed the symmetric temporal certificate and the final
   partition lost the split. Boundary evidence that supports a change did not
   establish that *removing* an existing split was safe. In the independently
   reviewed new temporal case, baseline and proposed remained unsplit, the
   half-beat probe did not activate, and exact source-first states did not
   match. No micro partition survived anywhere in the whole-file run.

Thus the remaining failure is **activation scope / temporal arbitration**,
not local candidate representability or local top-rank correction. The
production Family B helper and smoother are unchanged; their interaction
receives an unsafe Shadow arbitration decision. This classification is a
handoff for later human direction, not authorization to patch the gate now.
