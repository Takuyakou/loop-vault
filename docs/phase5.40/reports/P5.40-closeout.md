## What succeeded

Bounded candidate generation and semantic grammar expansion can represent
source-supported altered/omission-sensitive complex local identities and rank
the correct local candidate first. The final Stage02c evaluation retained
local generation **2/2** and local top rank **2/2**. Candidate visits remained
276–281 per rank call, below the hard limit of 300. Complex-identity local
representability is therefore not the principal remaining obstacle.

## What failed

**Activation scope and temporal arbitration remain unresolved.** Correct
local ranking activated too broadly across the whole file. Strict semantic
source evidence alone did not safely constrain activation. Ranking changes
affected temporal partition decisions; evidence that a boundary contains
change did not show that deleting an existing split was safe. Local
correctness and whole-file safety did not converge in the current Core.
