## Correction cost by checkpoint

The Stage 03 independent-Gold harness gives the note correction vector. Harmony dev has 164 missing and 54 extra note numbers at extraction; representation and persistence do not add loss. Card/capture playback drops exact matches from 31 to 25 of 160 and raises extras to 209. Validation extraction is 16/48 exact, while card/capture playback is 0/48. Copy-Oracle is 160/160 and 48/48 at every measured endpoint. Stage 01 role × boundary ablation separately shows that role evidence changes contamination; these costs are not added to the Gold-conditioned Stage 03 results because the arms have different conditioning.

The current product over-splits Harmony Gold even while recovering all Gold onset boundaries. Synthetic has both missing and extra boundaries and misses the one voicing-only change. Visible candidate exact-label recall is low on validation. The public `confidence` and alternative confidence values saturate on the Harmony cases: their measured mean difference is zero, and all events are classified as near ties under a 0.05 threshold. This is not a valid winner margin for architecture selection; later scorer experiments must use an unsaturated score. Timing quality, candidate reachability, and playback fidelity are separate loss axes. The correction vector counts are diagnostic operations, not a user-interaction-time estimate.

No private MIDI bytes, note rows, names, or checksums are tracked. No production path changed.
