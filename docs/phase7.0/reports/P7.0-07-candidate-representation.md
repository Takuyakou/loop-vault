# P7.0-07 — Candidate representation experiment

`scripts/p7/candidateRepresentations.ts` is an evaluation-only generator. It receives source MIDI pitch classes within a Gold event span, and does not receive Gold identity during generation. Gold span conditioning isolates candidate representation from temporal segmentation. Legacy-closed here is a **closed-template proxy**, not a rerun of the shipped legacy analyzer; it requires every observed pitch class to match a template exactly. Expanded-bounded tolerates one missing and one extra pitch class. Factorized represents root, quality, observed bass, and observed omission/extension flags separately. Shortcut-factorized reserves 32 places for simple matches plus 128 factorized tail entries. These are prototypes and are not wired into production.

Structural recall is exact root, quality, and bass against an independent Gold identity; factor tokens and actual renderer output are evaluated separately by the P7.0-04 Tier 2 contract. The candidate sets are bounded at 48, 96, 240, and 160 respectively, and ranked by one fixed pitch-set score. This scoring is only a local diagnostic. Runtime numbers in the JSONL aggregates are process measurements, not a robust 3/5/10 minute benchmark.

The closed proxy is intentionally stricter than the product template scorer; its poor recall is not a product baseline claim. The product displayed candidate diagnostic is in Stage 05. Factorized coverage improves before ranking, but expands false-candidate capacity to 240; this is a material runtime and decoder cost. Shortcut preserves reachability in these sets and lowers maximum candidates to 149, but does not eliminate ambiguity. Top1 remains poor on Harmony validation. These counts cannot establish Tier 2 semantic correctness or audible equivalence on their own.

The existing parser accepts 5 of the 6 synthetic primary Gold labels; explicit `no5` spelling is currently unsupported. The independent Gold identity is retained and that case is not silently renamed. Full aggregate-only outputs: `P7.0-07-candidates-dev.jsonl` and `P7.0-07-candidates-validation.jsonl`. The final sealed holdout has not been opened.
