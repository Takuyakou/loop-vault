# P7.0-08 — Boundary, role, and local score experiments

`scripts/p7/boundaryRoleScorer.ts` compares five source-only temporal proposals: integer-beat fixed grid; simultaneous onset clusters with at least two notes; half-beat pitch-class change points; a bounded union lattice; and the current product timeline. Gold boundaries are used only for scoring. The lattice is a proposal set, not a decoder; its false-boundary count is correspondingly high. The role arm applies the current Role v2 classifier as a hard melody/percussion filter, or retains uncertain melody voices as soft role evidence. Factorized candidates are ranked by either the Stage 07 overlap score or a local penalty for factor count. All changes remain evaluation-only.

The onset-cluster rule is highly effective on simultaneous synthetic chords and misses Harmony Gold boundaries entirely, showing a domain shift. Integer grid recovers all Harmony boundaries but strongly over-proposes and misses the half-beat synthetic chord. Lattice preserves short chords but needs a decoder or scorer to suppress extra boundaries. The dedicated test verifies that onset-cluster and lattice retain both entry and exit of the 1-beat and half-beat passing chords; duration alone never removes either.

Soft role improves dev Top3 but loses three validation Top3 while leaving Top1 unchanged there. The factor penalty has little effect. This is not sufficient to promote either component alone. The Gold-conditioned role/scorer experiment isolates those pieces from boundary choice. Full aggregate-only JSONL reports are tracked; no local real witness or sealed holdout was used.
