# P7.0-09 — Modular tournament and interaction comparison

## Predeclared matrix

23 configurations were executed on public-safe synthetic and independent Harmony Gold dev/validation: 12 product-boundary combinations of 2 roles × 2 identities × 3 decoders; 8 fixed-beat/lattice variants with C2; and Copy-Oracle, Copy-Simple, Copy-ProductBoundary. No configuration was eliminated after isolated results. `old Identity` reads the shipped analyzer's displayed chord and alternatives at each proposed segment midpoint. `new Identity` uses the bounded factorized candidate prototype. `old Decoder` is a research greedy adapter to the displayed top candidate, while C1 and C2 are research dynamic decoders: C1 adds a continuity cost, C2 reduces that cost where a simultaneous onset cluster supports a change. Thus old/old in this table is **not** a claim to reproduce all shipped production behavior. No production path was modified.

Every configuration reports source-note exact/F1, boundary hits and count, candidate reachability/Top3, decoded structural Top1, note correction counts, card-count delta, passing-chord preservation, per-run wall elapsed time, observed heap usage, and maximum candidate count in the aggregate-only JSONL. Tier 2 in this matrix is root/quality/bass structural accuracy against independent Gold labels; the Stage 04 accepted-alternative/playback-equivalence contract remains separate and is not collapsed into this proxy. Copy arms have no chord identity, so their Tier 2 fields are unavailable, not zero accuracy. Runtime/heap of copy arms were not measured by this harness. A deterministic-repeat test compares every non-telemetry metric.

The modular note-copy prototype improves synthetic fidelity with lattice and preserves both 1-beat and half-beat passing chord boundaries. On Harmony Gold, every non-oracle prototype has zero exact note sets and trails the shipped Gold-conditioned extractor from Stage 03 (31/160 dev and 16/48 validation exact at extraction). Therefore **no modular candidate is ready for Core v2 promotion**. This is a real cross-domain regression, not a reason to stop the research. The note source/role selection must be redesigned before Phase 8 production work.

## Identity × decoder interaction

On product boundaries with hard role, current candidate reachability is 80/160 dev and 10/48 validation; factorized candidates reach 146/160 and 42/48 respectively. The old-identity greedy adapter gets 25/160 and 0/48 decoded structural Top1. C2 raises old identity to 44/160 and 9/48, showing a positive decoder interaction. New identity greedy gets 19/160 and 0/48; new identity C2 reaches 46/160 but remains 0/48 validation. C1 contributes little in these runs. Factorized recall therefore does not guarantee a useful decoder result. The required new Identity × C1/C2 arms were executed despite poor isolated validation Top1.

Lattice plus soft role reaches all 140/140 Harmony dev and 42/42 validation Gold boundary positions, but proposes 889 and 302 boundaries respectively; that count is a card-count/correction cost regression without stronger temporal selection. Synthetic lattice preserves both short passing-chord categories (2/2) while product boundaries preserve neither. Product hard-role current identity uses at most six displayed alternatives; factorized generation reaches up to 240 candidates per segment. The largest measured modular dev arm took under one second in this local run, but repeated source parsing and allocator effects make these figures unsuitable as 3/5/10-minute capacity proof. Heap readings are process snapshots, not isolated peak allocations.

Full rows for all 23 arms are in `P7.0-09-tournament-dev.jsonl` and `P7.0-09-tournament-validation.jsonl`. Synthetic is the same control in both. No sealed holdout answer, private real-MIDI human Gold, or old evaluation result was used to create truth. The next stage can shortlist mechanisms for Phase 8 research, while the final sealed check must not be used for retuning.
