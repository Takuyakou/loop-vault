## Findings and answers

1. **Syntax families:** 18 confirmed neutral families (see `03-syntax-saturation-report.md`).
2. **Public-example-only syntax:** 0 families lacked source corroboration. Public examples uniquely revealed structural usage absent from the official sample, including repeated/edge delimiters and full-width spacing; no unsupported form was promoted solely from a user score.
3. **Official/current/legacy difference:** The current official sample teaches the main controls, comments, slash bass, and compact input. Current source additionally recognizes `N.C.`, `on` bass, alternate separators, and playback markers. A historical article and release history explain older whitespace behavior; they do not override current source. The local historical P5.20 prose rejecting `%` conflicts with current product code, which accepts it.
4. **Saturation:** reached for this bounded inventory after two consecutive zero-new-syntax and zero-new-structure passes, plus one extra check. The 140-score sample is time-sorted, so this is not universal coverage.
5. **UNKNOWN/AMBIGUOUS:** seven recorded issues cover compact segmentation, root/type spacing, inline metadata, duplicate delimiters, Unicode punctuation, version-dependent whitespace, and the local `%` contract conflict. They require diagnostics or explicit resolution.
6. **Canonical fixtures:** 98 authored synthetic cases; 71 positive, 22 negative, 2 ambiguous, 3 empty. Version `extended-v1-canonical-v1` is frozen by manifest digest.
7. **Property/fuzz:** fixed-seed runtime generator, 14 mutation families and 200 in-memory cases; assert no crash/hang/silent loss, raw preservation, valid spans, determinism, atomic conversion.
8. **Standard/extended overlap:** `%`, `_`, `=`, ASCII `|`, line-leading comments, slash bass, compact labels, and limited root/type spacing already exist. Reuse one chord lexer/semantic parser and separate structural/timing adapters.
9. **Semantic gaps:** in the 30-label synthetic audit, four Product parse gaps (`mMaj7`, omit, add13, power chord) and three pitch-class disagreements (11, 13, combined alterations). External 6/9 translation failed separately.
10. **Chord family understanding:** 26/30 labels parsed and factorized; parse success is insufficient because three both-supported labels had different reference/product pitch classes. No silent approximation.
11. **Slash bass:** bass pitch class agreed in all 25 both-supported labels. `6/9` must stay a quality slash, and `on` must normalize only as an unambiguous bass alias. Exact bass octave is a separate voicing policy.
12. **External voicing complexity:** `SIMPLE_CHORD_LOCAL` for the inspected public source, with live deployment parity still to verify.
13. **Same chord/concrete notes:** the inspected conversion path uses no neighboring chord, so the same chord and settings yield the same note set. This is a source-path result, not a direct live deployment guarantee.
14. **Difference from Product Generated:** 0/25 shared synthetic cases matched exact notes under generated-close, shell, or open. Bass pitch class often matches; register, duplication, cardinality, and three chord interpretations differ.
15. **Exact reproduction:** feasible to prototype because the inspected rule is small and deterministic; not yet proven desirable by listening or compatible with all chord meanings.
16. **Dedicated policy:** prototype an optional, neutral reference-voicing policy in Phase 8.5; promote only after live verification and audible/user benefit versus current Generated styles. No policy was implemented in PRE.
17. **Persistence:** represent concrete text-derived notes as `TEXT_REFERENCE` or equivalent with policy/version and written identity; never label them SOURCE MIDI or fabricate manual capture. Vault v2 needs an explicit preservation design before persistence.
18. **Phase 8.5 stages:** (a) shared lexical/semantic parser and diagnostics; (b) extended structural/timing adapter and metadata; (c) raw-source-preserving Draft/Vault design with migration review; (d) preview/save/Voicing Loop integration; (e) optional reference-voicing prototype; (f) fixture/property, live, privacy, rollback and product acceptance. Do not change unrelated Phase 9 or Analyzer code.
19. **Human judgment:** choose accepted 11th/13th/altered equivalence, whether exact external-sounding reference policy is worth shipping, and any persistence model/schema migration. These do not undo the PRE research freeze.
20. **Copyright/privacy:** only short authored synthetic fixtures, aggregate public-pattern counts, neutral behavior descriptions, and research scripts are tracked. Raw public scores, external source clones, per-example comparison notes, and source-specific mappings remain ignored local-only. No private MIDI or audio was used.
