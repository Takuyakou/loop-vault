# P8.8.1-00 — Root-cause audit

## Reproduction

Using only authored synthetic compact bars, the current Extended parser
rejects all four supplied compound two-bar examples before constructing any
slots. A spaced eight-slot control reaches the timing check and is rejected
with UNSUPPORTED_RHYTHM. No real chart content was inspected or logged.

## Distinct causes

1. Shared score segmentation searches at most four compact tokens per bar in
   textScoreTokenizer.ts. That is why supplied adjacent eight-slot bars fail
   with generic INVALID_CHORD or MALFORMED_SLASH before timing is reached.
2. extendedTextProgression.ts requires beatsPerBar modulo tokenCount to equal
   zero. A valid 4/4 eight-slot bar therefore fails even when lexically spaced.
3. Extended imports Standard's 8192-code-unit, 32-bar and 128-token limits.
   Vault's explicit-score save guard independently repeats the 32-bar,
   128-token and integer-beat assumptions. The strict optional textSource
   schema caps slots/spans at 128 and position at 384 beats.
4. Isolated F7-5, Fm7-5, F7+5, F7+9 and F7+11 labels are rejected by
   parseTextChordLabel, independent of bar timing. C+ and Caug/D parse.
   G#m7 parses as minor seventh but canonicalizes its display to Abm7,
   losing the written sharp spelling in the preview and saved chord label.
5. The shared practice grid uses PPQ 192. Halves, thirds, quarters, sixths,
   eighths, twelfths and sixteenths of a beat can be represented only when
   their resulting denominator divides 192. Fifth subdivisions require an
   exact parser source representation and an explicit downstream conversion
   or unsupported-practice decision.
6. Current diagnostics combine lexical, semantic, structural and timing
   failures into one generic message. The input retains raw invalid bar
   text, while the preview dropped the failed bar and did not identify its cause.
