## PRE reconciliation

- The earlier 11th and 13th disagreements were based on a smaller historical-source sample. Current deployed evidence confirms a stacked-site 13th including natural 11, while the independent contract makes natural 11 optional. Minor 11 explicitly requires the 11th. Thus an unqualified “semantic error” statement for those symbols is too broad; the current matrix records the exact surface and policy involved.
- The earlier combined-alteration finding remains relevant, but the current-site translator's same-degree overwrite and Product audition truncation are distinct mechanisms. The matrix records each written operator and each actual audition surface separately.
- PRE's `0/25` exact concrete-note result applies to its historical-reference/generated-close synthetic sample. R05 measured current Text Preview/Vault and `basic-full` Voicing Loop on a different frozen corpus; these are different policies and denominators. The old count must not be carried forward as a current-site whole-vocabulary rate.
- The historical `SIMPLE_CHORD_LOCAL` conclusion now has direct deployed-source and live UI corroboration at the pinned site revision. It must be rechecked after any site asset change.
- EV1-070–EV1-077 are prior semantic errata, not editable expected values in this audit. The frozen 98-case PRE fixture and digest remained unchanged.
