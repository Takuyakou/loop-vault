## Correction Editor root cause and scope

At the failing 1280×720 browser viewport, the selected candidate row had 601px of space and used a wrapping flex layout. Its title button had `flex-basis: 0%`, `min-width: 0`, and `flex-grow: 1`. The action group occupied about 356px, the occurrence text 180px, and the two gaps 24px. Flex wrapping treated the title's zero basis as fitting on the same row, leaving **41.046875px** for its rendered width. Candidate text therefore wrapped into a thin vertical strip. The issue was the title item's flex basis and minimum width, not a grid `minmax()` rule or a fixed viewport limit.

The isolated product change in `src/views/CaptureView.tsx` gives that title button a 10rem flex basis and minimum width (`basis-40 min-w-40`). When sibling content cannot fit, the existing `flex-wrap` moves it to the next line while preserving the title's readable width. No fixed card width was added. The existing browser assertion remains **at least 160px**; it was not lowered or skipped. No P8.8.6 Text Capture, Core, Vault, Parser, or Transport product code was changed by this fix.
