# P9.1 — Source Independence Research

## Decision

**P9.1 RESEARCH COMPLETE / SOURCE-INDEPENDENT CANDIDATE NO-GO FOR PROMOTION.** The frozen onset-support shadow policy does not improve matched Tier 1 exactness or satisfy the frozen missing/extra guardrails. Current Product source extraction, Identity/Decoder, Vault v2, and production defaults remain unchanged. P9.1-P was not started.

## Frozen method and provenance

The research selector in [sourceIndependent.ts](../../../scripts/p9/sourceIndependent.ts) accepts raw timed notes, PPQ, a supplied source window, and source-derived voice roles. It has no chord identity, analyzer candidate, score, rank, or old Gold input. The evaluator first selects note numbers; a separate research adapter then stamps the matched chord key to exercise the unchanged Vault v2 and playback contracts. Selection used only public dev. Four bounded methods were compared: densest simultaneous cluster, onset plus already-sounding support, duration-weighted support, and role-aware interval union. The best total dev exact count was onset-support (72/221), versus matched Product 88/221. [The policy freeze](../evidence/dev-policy-freeze.json) was committed at **e566945ddf82e304a8d184463267384330d70f7b** before validation; the frozen method was evaluated on public Harmony validation **once** and was not retuned.

Each A/B pair has identical corpus ID/version, manifest SHA, split, code commit, policy ID, boundary source, identity source, scoring contract, and metric version. Only snapshot source differs. The repository provenance validator reports **13/13 COMPARABLE**. Temporal and Harmony use Gold boundaries plus Gold identity; usage v2 uses Gold windows plus the same Product-predicted identity in both arms. Cross-corpus and cross-identity headline numbers are not treated as matched comparisons.

## Failure isolation and correction cost

The public raw source windows contain **all** Gold note numbers: temporal 83/83, usage 153/153, Harmony dev 752/752, and Harmony validation 192/192. Among simultaneous source-only candidates, an exact Gold note set exists in 21/22, 37/39, 64/160, and 42/48 events respectively. Thus Harmony dev has 96 events without an exact simultaneous candidate; among the 64 reachable events, the frozen selector chooses exactly 16. Harmony validation has 6 without an exact simultaneous candidate and 30 reachable but not selected. These are candidate representation and selection failures, not raw note evidence absence. This is a bounded candidate diagnostic, not proof that every unreachable event needs an entirely new algorithm.

The Gold-lowest-note presence proxy misses zero events for both arms on these corpora. It is not the full bass-role or defining-tone contract. The note edit vector (missing, extra, event sets requiring correction) is temporal Product (0,1,1) vs shadow (0,1,1); usage (0,6,3) vs (0,7,4); Harmony dev (164,54,129) vs (192,99,144); validation (42,32,32) vs (0,36,36). These values are one-add/one-remove note-set costs, not human interaction time.

The extraction-only measured times on this host were at most 5 ms per corpus for Product and 4 ms for the independent selector; sampled peak heap ranged about 179–245 MiB. They are not the frozen 10-minute workload benchmark and cannot clear the resource promotion gate. Review precision/recall/false-alarm are **unavailable** because the candidate does not emit comparable ReviewReason predictions. Independent defining-tone Gold is **unavailable** pending the full factor contract. Product-boundary behavior and operational card inflation were not evaluated because boundaries were held to Gold. No private witness or sealed holdout was opened.

## Promotion guardrails

- Tier 1 improvement: **FAIL** on usage, Harmony dev, and Harmony validation; tie only on temporal.
- Public Gold note recall: **FAIL** on Harmony dev (560/752 versus Product 588/752); temporal and usage tie. Harmony validation improves recall but not exact.
- Missing support: **FAIL** on Harmony dev (192 vs 164 missing Gold notes). Independent support-role recall is not separately computed.
- Extra note non-regression: **FAIL** on usage (7 vs 6), Harmony dev (99 vs 54), and validation (36 vs 32). Extra per 100 Gold is approximately 4.58, 13.16, and 18.75 against the frozen split ceilings 3.93, 7.19, and 16.67.
- Bass presence proxy: 0 missing in both arms. Full bass/defining-tone factors remain unavailable.
- Vault/Card/Capture/Whole source-note set: **PASS** for no additional loss relative to each arm's extraction output.
- Current MIDI modes, human review metrics, actual audio timing, 10-minute resource gate, private catastrophic regression, and rollback in Product: **NOT EVALUATED**. Their absence is not treated as a PASS.

Therefore the candidate is frozen as a research NO-GO; no Product switch or P9.1-P work follows. Future independent selection work would need a richer candidate representation and a selector that does not overinclude distractors, assessed on expanded public dev rather than this validation or private witness.
