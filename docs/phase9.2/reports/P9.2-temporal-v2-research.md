# P9.2 — Temporal v2 generalization and bounded selection

## Decision

**P9.2 RESEARCH COMPLETE / TEMPORAL v2 CANDIDATE NO-GO FOR PROMOTION.** Separate Harmonic Boundary, Voicing Boundary, and Ornament/Note Event heads work on the small authored corpus, but the frozen bounded selector loses many public Harmony Gold boundaries and exceeds the 10% harmonic card-inflation guardrail. Keep the P8.5/P9.2 research result; do not change the Product analyzer, default boundary path, Vault, or Identity/Decoder.

## Method, freeze, and provenance

The retained P8.5 three-head proposal model was ported into [research-only temporalV2.ts](../../../scripts/p9/temporalV2.ts). [The source adapter](../../../scripts/p9/temporalSourceEvidence.ts) reads raw note number, onset, offset, pedal sustain, re-strike, PPQ, and source meter; neither proposal nor selector receives Gold, chord identity, Analyzer score, or candidate rank. The high-recall lattice includes source onsets, offsets, and half-beat positions. A bounded STRUCTURAL selector filters by onset and sounding-set changes without any minimum chord duration. P7 authored temporal dev, six new scenario-diverse authored dev files, and Harmony primary dev alone informed the selector choice. The candidate was frozen at **b094021c4545db516f622f6188dc7cb89e795a90** before a single evaluation of authored and Harmony validation. No validation retuning occurred.

The verified public Harmony corpus contains **20 primary dev, 4 primary validation, and 2 diagnostic-only validation files**; it has no diagnostic-only dev files. Harmony Harmonic Gold boundaries are transitions where consecutive independent manifest chord identities differ. Voicing Gold boundaries are transitions where consecutive independent Gold note-number sets differ. Harmony has no independently labeled Ornament/Note Event Gold; that head is not scored there. The authored corpus carries separate head labels. Product provides a Harmonic comparison only.

Every aggregate has corpus ID/version, manifest SHA, split, code commit, method-specific policy ID, boundary source, identity source, snapshot source, scoring contract, and metric version. The provenance validator finds **5/5 intended Product versus STRUCTURAL comparisons comparable** when policy ID, boundary source, and identity source are explicitly designated as the ablation. The public source bytes, PPQ, and meter passed invariance checks on **35/35 dev files and 10/10 validation files**.

The P9.2 selector preserves both one-beat and half-beat passing-chord boundaries in P7 dev (2/2 files), plus the authored 1/4-meter offbeat half-beat passing case (1/1 file). It leaves a re-strike and ghost bass as Note Events rather than harmonic cards in the synthetic regressions. This improvement on small authored cases does not generalize to Harmony. On Harmony dev the selector reduces false harmonic proposals from Product's 160 to 78 but misses 58/140 Gold boundaries; its harmonic card inflation is **12.5%**, above the frozen **10%** limit. On Harmony validation it misses 19/42, has 37 false boundaries, and card inflation rises to **37.5%**. The Product comparison has high over-segmentation of its own; lower false proposals cannot offset missing Gold boundaries.

The lattice is a recall ceiling: it hits all Gold harmonic boundaries in each measured cohort but makes 2,737 proposals on Harmony dev and 729 on Harmony validation. It is not a card plan. Near-duplicate lattice proposals can fall within the one-tick matching tolerance of the same Gold boundary; lattice false/correction counts in the machine-readable evidence are lower bounds by 8 on Harmony dev and 1 on validation. Bounded STRUCTURAL and Product rows above have no such duplicate remainder.

## Primary versus diagnostic validation

| Harmony validation subset | Method | Hit / Gold | False | Correction cost | Cards |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Primary, 4 files | Product | 28/28 | 32 | 32 | 64/32 |
| Primary, 4 files | P9.2 structural | 14/28 | 31 | 45 | 49/32 |
| Diagnostic-only, 2 files | Product | 14/14 | 14 | 14 | 30/16 |
| Diagnostic-only, 2 files | P9.2 structural | 9/14 | 6 | 11 | 17/16 |

The diagnostic-only subset lowers correction cost and card inflation to 6.25%, but recall falls to 9/14. The primary subset loses half its Gold boundaries and has 53.1% card inflation. Neither subset clears a generalization or recall decision.

Aggregate feature isolation explains the recall ceiling of the current bounded selector. On Harmony dev, 66 lattice rows near Gold boundaries have no onset at that boundary; another 82 have exactly one onset. The strict structural selector requires an onset and cannot recover the zero-onset group. This is a source-evidence timing limitation, consistent with delayed support and staggered onset, rather than proof that those Gold boundaries are unknowable. A future method needs context around a boundary; adding a metrical split without corroborating evidence would risk new false cards. No post-validation rule was added.

## Runtime and limitations

[The public offline benchmark](../evidence/public-resource.json) includes source evidence construction, lattice proposals, and all three bounded selectors. At 10 minutes/120 BPM with 4,800 public synthetic notes it took **174.55 ms** and sampled about **181 MiB heapUsed**; at 10 minutes/240 BPM with 9,600 notes it took **506.53 ms** and about **175 MiB**. These runs are below the frozen 1,200 ms warning, 3,000 ms hard limit, and 512 MiB heap limit on this host, but they are not a whole Product import or audio latency measurement. The benchmark uses repeated synthetic chords and is not evidence of diverse real-MIDI resource performance.

The source adapter preserves raw note-number and onset/offset tick facts in deterministic tests and does not alter MIDI bytes. Actual Card/Capture/Whole playback, source-note selection, Identity/Decoder, full Tier 2, and real local witness acceptance are outside P9.2 boundary research. Private LF-MIDI-001 and the sealed holdout were not used. The Harmony manifest permits a structural proxy for Harmonic/Voicing boundaries; it does not provide a full three-head temporal Gold or independently labeled Note Events.
