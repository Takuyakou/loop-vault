# P9.3 — Source Extraction v2 research

## Decision

**P9.3 RESEARCH COMPLETE / SOURCE EXTRACTION v2 CANDIDATE NO-GO FOR PROMOTION.** Reversible raw-note evidence and a richer bounded candidate pool are retained as research. The dev-frozen safe policy reproduces Product extraction exactly and gives no Tier 1 gain; source-only ranking and low-velocity pruning raise missing support/Gold notes. Product Core C, the extractor, Identity/Decoder, Vault v2 and defaults stay unchanged. P9.4 was not started.

## Entry and matched comparison

P9.0/P9.1 established a material extraction bottleneck: in public Harmony dev, all 752 Gold note-number occurrences are present in raw source windows, yet Product selection keeps 588 and misses 164. The P9.1 simultaneous candidate ceiling is 64/160 exact events. P9.2's temporal candidate did not generalize, so P9.3 uses independent Gold windows to isolate source extraction; it does not claim end-to-end boundary accuracy.

All A/B arms in each row use the same public MIDI, boundary, and identity. P7 temporal and Harmony use Gold identity; Usage v2 uses the **same Product-predicted identity** in every arm. These identity conditions are not combined into a single operational ranking. The machine-readable artifacts carry corpus ID/version, manifest SHA, split, code commit, policy ID, boundary/identity/snapshot source, scoring contract and metric version. The comparison checker finds Product versus each research arm **COMPARABLE** with only snapshot source as an intentional difference. Harmony source bytes are verified against the versioned manifest; its holdout is not opened.

The research-only extractor stores every raw note with a stable local ID, MIDI number, onset/offset ticks, velocity, track and channel. It proposes simultaneous, onset-plus-delayed, relative interval-support, soft-role and track-group note sets, plus an explicitly labeled Product anchor. A candidate score and bounded selector operate without Gold. Short passing harmony is not removed by a whole-event duration cutoff. Every excluded note retains an ID in Source Truth and is restorable in memory; no post-save excluded-note contract is claimed before P9.8. The chosen **PRODUCT_GUARDED** policy must include all Product-selected pitches and was frozen at **d02a320e9234ed1eb237c0f0f330303692478d19** after public dev. Its freeze status was already `NO_GO_ON_DEV`. Public validation was run once per available cohort after that commit, with no retuning.

The Product-guarded arm has no measured Tier 1 gain in any cohort. Its Harmony correction proxy is 218 on dev and 74 on validation, exactly Product's values. Source-only rank improves Usage exact but loses 2/153 authored support notes; its Harmony dev missing count grows by 214. Safe-prune removes four Usage extras without new support loss, yet loses 171 additional Harmony dev Gold notes and 50 validation Gold notes. A low-velocity, short-overlap signal is therefore unsafe as a general support-note deletion rule. All arms have zero Gold-lowest-pitch misses; this is only a bass-presence proxy, not independent bass-role Gold. Defining-tone Gold and a full Tier 2 factor scorer are unavailable, so their no-increase guardrail cannot be certified.

Usage category breakdown is in the machine aggregate: source-only rank improves clean/transcription exact but loses two notes in the instrument category. The Harmony artifacts separate clean and stress variants. The current evaluator does not further split Harmony validation primary versus diagnostic modes; no mode-specific promotion claim is made.

The candidate pool includes the Product anchor, so candidate exact is an **ensemble** ceiling, not a pure source-only recall score. The Harmony dev gap includes 96 events without any exact candidate, then 33 exact candidates not selected by Product-guarded. In validation all 48 events have an exact candidate but 32 are not selected by the frozen policy. This is a candidate representation problem on dev and a selection problem on validation; it is not raw note-number absence. The dramatic split difference also rules out tuning to validation. The research selector does not output comparable ReviewReason flags, so review precision/recall/false-alarm remain unavailable.

## Source, round trip and resource gates

All public files retained byte hash and parsed note-number/onset/offset facts: **55/55 dev files and 10/10 validation files**. All excluded-note references in the research arms resolve to retained Source Truth IDs; all reported research exclusions are restorable in memory. A public synthetic SourceSnapshot test passes Vault v2 save/reload and Card, Capture and Whole playback-plan note-number equality for the selected set. This is one controlled product-function round trip, not a complete corpus-wide audio or UI acceptance gate. Meter/timing after Vault reload, real audio output, Product-boundary end-to-end results and post-save restoration of excluded notes were not evaluated.

The public offline 1/4-meter repeated-chord benchmark runs source evidence, candidates and Product-guarded selection for each interval. At 10 minutes/120 BPM (4,800 source notes, 1,200 beats) it took **281.91 ms**, with **119.03 MiB** maximum sampled Node heapUsed. At 10 minutes/240 BPM (9,600 notes, 2,400 beats) it took **1,121.45 ms**, with **125.37 MiB** sampled heapUsed. Both are under the 1,200 ms warning, 3,000 ms hard and 512 MiB sampled heap limits on this host, but the workload is repetitive and excludes the Product analyzer, Vault and audio UI. It cannot establish resource performance on diverse real MIDI.

The short half-beat passing chord, delayed arpeggio support, guarded Product-note preservation, and excluded-note restoration have deterministic tests. No private MIDI, human Gold, or sealed holdout was used for method selection or evaluation. The frozen P8.4 candidate remains NO-GO; its private Human Gold was not used as a tuning set.
