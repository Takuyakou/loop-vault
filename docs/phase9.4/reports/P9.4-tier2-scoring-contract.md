## Scoring contract

The scorer reports canonical factorized identity, accepted alternate identity, structural root/quality/bass, factors, label exact, Top1/Top3/bounded/full candidate recall, and an explicit disposition. `CANONICAL`, `ACCEPTED_ALTERNATE`, `NOTATION_ONLY`, `SAME_SOUND_DIFFERENT_MEANING`, `DEFINING_TONE_LOSS`, `OPTIONAL_TONE_OMISSION`, `RENDERER_MISMATCH`, `UNSUPPORTED_VOCABULARY`, `AMBIGUOUS_UNKNOWN`, `UNKNOWN_INCORRECT`, `SEMANTIC_MISMATCH` and `IDENTITY_UNAVAILABLE` remain distinct. Root and observed bass are independent fields. Pitch-class plus bass agreement, defining-tone loss and optional-tone omission require candidate renderer or Product playback notes. If no such notes are supplied, these metrics are `null` and measured coverage is zero; Gold source notes are never substituted as candidate playback.

The six P7 authored examples cover C6/Am7/C equivalence, rootless C9, omitted fifth, altered dominant, thirteenth and slash bass. Eight focused tests verify semantic versus sound separation, true candidate-rendered playback, missing-renderer availability, UNKNOWN, vocabulary, omissions and ranking cutoffs. The tests are diagnostic cases, not a representative performance corpus.

## Limits and next research seam

The authored Gold has only four dev and two validation examples; its role pitch classes do not cover real transcription ambiguity. Notation-only classification is a semantic identity plus display-label diagnostic when renderer output is absent; audible equivalence remains unmeasured. UNKNOWN/unsupported handling is tested synthetically but cannot be population-estimated from the six resolved examples. There is no Tier 1 register/timing, correction-time, Product operational identity, or decoder re-score in this stage. No ranking weights, decoder policy, Product code, Vault format, default or promotion decision changed.

P9.5 should expand independently authored scenario diversity and accepted alternatives, obtain actual candidate-renderer outputs, keep source/boundary/identity provenance matched, then study local ranking. Decoder necessity should be revisited only after ranking. This report does not authorize P9.5.
