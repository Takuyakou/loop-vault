# P9.5 — Candidate Ranking v2 research

## Decision

**P9.5 RESEARCH COMPLETE / LOCAL RANKING v2 NO-GO FOR PROMOTION.** A dev-frozen pitch+bass rerank improves Harmony's structural root/quality/bass proxy, but it does not improve accepted full Tier 2 Top1 on the authored corpus. The full factorized arms also fail to improve authored Top1, and the sparse UNKNOWN rule misses most review-worthy errors. Keep the research feature seam and fixed candidate generator; retain current Product Core C, extractor, Identity/Decoder and Vault v2. No decoder decision or Product switch is made here.

## Design and freeze

The research ranker takes the same P8.6 shortcut-factorized candidates for every arm. It adds distinct pitch-overlap, observed bass, track-role proxy, overlap duration, raw onset confidence, defining-degree presence, short tension/non-chord-tone penalty, vocabulary prior, and previous **source-only** local Top1 context terms. The old overlap score itself includes a bass component; the `PITCH_BASS` ablation therefore tests an additional bass term, not a clean orthogonal decomposition. Track-role is a low-track proxy, not independent role Gold. Prior context is never supplied from Gold. A full onset cluster suppresses context stickiness, including half-beat passing harmony. No duration cutoff discards short chords. Sparse observations below three distinct pitch classes produce UNKNOWN with retained Top-K in non-OLD arms; no margin threshold was calibrated.

The [dev policy freeze](../evidence/dev-policy-freeze.json) was committed as **cbcadf8ca6f625bc21396a99cacd48fa030b9ffc** before the single public validation evaluation. Dev chose `PITCH_BASS` for diagnostic comparison: accepted authored Top1 tied at 11/18 across arms, while Harmony structural Top1 was 72/160 versus OLD 68/160 and factorized 71/160. This is **not** a promotion selection. No policy, threshold, scenario or generator was changed after validation.

Public authored Gold adds block, seventh, diminished, suspended, extension, inversion, rootless, half-beat passing, pedal, staggered arpeggio, short ghost bass and melody passing scenarios beyond the original six P7 harmonic examples. The split has 18 resolved dev and 9 resolved validation cases, plus 3/2 authored insufficient-context or cluster UNKNOWN controls. These are diverse synthetic scenarios, not transpose multiplication or human real-MIDI Gold. Their labels and accepted factors are authored in [the versioned fixture](../../../scripts/p9/rankingAuthoredGold.ts), not inferred from ranker output. Harmony's 160/48 events have independently versioned chordSymbol but lack accepted alternatives and role/factor adjudication; Harmony is a **structural diagnostic only**, under raw source windows at authored boundaries. This snapshot input differs from P8.6's Product-conditioned P8.4 candidate snapshot; therefore its counts must not be compared numerically with P8.6's 156/160 or 43/48 candidate recall. All arms within *this* comparison have matched boundary, source, candidate generator and split.

On dev, 3/18 authored events have **candidate misses** before ranking; of the 15 with an accepted candidate, 4 remain wrong at Top1. On validation the corresponding counts are 1/9 candidate miss and 1/8 rankable error. Ghost bass and a short extra melody note can make the true factorized identity absent when candidates use every raw pitch and the lowest observed pitch as bass. Ranking cannot fix these representation misses. The authored half-beat passing chord remains Top1 in both splits for all arms; there is no evidence here that context merges it. Harmony clean/stress validation structural Top1 for PITCH_BASS is 6/24 and 5/24, versus OLD 0/24 and 3/24. These are root/quality/bass exact, not accepted-equivalence rates. The category counts are in the aggregates.

The correction-event proxy is unchanged at 7/18 dev and 2/9 validation for all ranking arms. It counts non-accepted Top1, not user edit time. On authored Gold, PITCH_BASS correct and wrong Top1 margins are both about 0.12 on dev, so margin does not separate errors reliably and no UNKNOWN margin threshold is justified. Sparse UNKNOWN correctly flags 2/3 dev and 1/2 validation controls, with zero authored false alarms. When ranking errors on resolved cases are also included, review-flag proxy precision is 1.00 but recall is only **0.20 dev / 0.25 validation**, below the predeclared 0.90 threshold. This is a deterministic proxy, **not measured human ReviewReason precision/recall**. The non-sparse chromatic cluster remains unresolved by this rule. No independent real-world UNKNOWN calibration is available.

## Resource and limitations

The [public 10-minute benchmark](../evidence/public-resource.json) processes 1,200 one-beat source windows in 1/4 meter with 178,800 ranked candidates per arm. The frozen PITCH_BASS arm took **306.18 ms**, with **24.26 MiB** maximum sampled Node heapUsed. This passes the ranking-only local budget but excludes candidate generation, analyzer, Vault, playback and UI; it is not an end-to-end resource Gate. Focused tests ensure membership preservation, half-beat retention, sparse UNKNOWN Top-K, ghost-bass miss isolation and source-only context.

The corpus is small for full Tier 2, the Harmony proxy does not contain accepted alternatives, and candidate-renderer playback remains unavailable. Defining-tone loss, optional-tone omission in actual playback, measured human review effort, Product-boundary performance, and real-MIDI generalization remain unmeasured. P9.4's scorer is used only where authored accepted identities exist. Neither private LF-MIDI-001 nor sealed holdout was read or tuned. The P9.5 factorized features remain research code, and the PITCH_BASS diagnostic improvement is insufficient to advance Identity/Decoder. P9.6 requires a separate assignment and a promotion contract frozen before any decoder comparison.
