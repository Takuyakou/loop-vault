## 1. Oracle bottleneck analysis

### Measurement contracts

The [P7 boundary/role ablation](../phase7.0/reports/P7.0-01-oracle-ablation.md) holds **Gold identity in every arm**. It uses independent Harmony note-role and note-set Gold; the baseline in the next table is Product boundary + Product role **conditional on Gold identity**, not operational Current Product Core. P9.0's [Product baseline](P9.0-final-report.md) separately records Product-predicted structural identity (48/160 dev, 14/48 validation) under Gold windows. There is no measured same-event, same-code full accepted Tier 2 end-to-end ladder joining these sources. Its value is `NOT_MEASURED`, not an interpolated score.

Tier 1 exact counts entire Gold note-number sets. “Note edits” below are missing + extra note occurrences, not human actions. Boundary corrections are missed Gold boundaries + false proposed boundaries. Gold note-set substitution is a **ceiling by construction** and does not demonstrate that a Product algorithm can infer the set. The public Harmony Gold roles are track/note facts; no role proxy is promoted to Gold. Full accepted-equivalence Tier 2 is available only in the separate small P9.5 authored identity cohort.

### Separate candidate/ranking ceiling, not stitched into the ladder

P9.3's SourceSnapshot pool reaches 64/160 Harmony dev and 48/48 validation events; Product-guarded selection reaches 31/160 and 16/48. Therefore dev has 96 candidate misses and 33 selection misses; validation has 0 candidate misses and 32 selection misses **within this source-snapshot research pool**. All 752/752 dev and 192/192 validation Gold note occurrences exist in the raw source windows. This identifies a representation/selection bottleneck without pretending that raw presence is an exact candidate.

P9.5 uses a different, factorized **identity** generator and independent authored accepted-equivalence Gold. Its OLD research arm has 11/18 dev and 7/9 validation accepted Top1. Accepted candidate recall is 15/18 and 8/9, giving a Gold-choice-on-existing-candidates ceiling of +4 dev and +1 validation accepted Top1; 3/18 and 1/9 are candidate misses that ranking cannot fix. This is a candidate-constrained identity oracle, **not** a Current Product ranking result or a Tier 1 SourceSnapshot candidate. Correction-event proxy would fall from 7/18→3/18 dev and 2/9→1/9 validation if Gold selected whenever reachable; those are oracle lower bounds, not achievable UX savings. Product full Tier 2 candidate/ranking ablations remain `NOT_MEASURED`.

**Largest measured Product-adjacent opportunity:** on Harmony, extraction is far below its raw-source ceiling (31/160 dev, 16/48 validation under Gold windows/identity). Role oracle removes large contamination, but dev still misses 162 notes and achieves only 64/160 after both boundary and role oracles. Source candidates themselves cover only 64/160 dev. Boundary oversegmentation adds 160/46 merge-like corrections yet does not explain the conditional exact extraction deficit. The P9.5 identity funnel has a smaller, separately measured 4/18 and 1/9 rankable Top1 gap. These are different denominators; no numeric cross-cohort ROI ratio is claimed.

## 4. Why this decision

P9.1 and P9.3 source candidates fail matched Tier 1 non-regression; P9.2 improves authored boundaries but loses 58/140 dev and 19/42 validation Harmony boundaries; P9.5 leaves accepted Top1 and correction-event proxy unchanged. Selective automation lacks a validated, runtime-observable trigger. A broad redesign may be warranted later, but its measured headroom is an oracle upper bound and its cost/risk is much greater than the evidence presently supports. Current playback and Vault paths do not add further note-set loss in the P9.1 matched public checks, so helping users correct selected source and boundaries is the most bounded next investment hypothesis.

The recommendation is **A**, subject to a separate implementation assignment and measured interaction-cost baseline. It does not promote a P9.1–P9.5 component, start P9.6, switch Product defaults, change Vault schema, or close Phase 9. A future decision can revisit B/C after public scenario diversity and a genuinely matched full Tier 2 Product ladder exist. P9.6 remains unrun with its formal comparison opportunity intact.
