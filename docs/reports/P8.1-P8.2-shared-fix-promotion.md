## LF-MIDI-001 read-only smoke

The **current Product extractor** yielded 25 cards with source snapshots. Save, reload, Card, Capture and Whole each retained source note-number sets **25/25**; save/reload/Whole timing each matched **25/25**. Meter and source bytes were unchanged. This is self-consistency, not independent Human Gold accuracy. P8.4 was not enabled. Only aggregates were emitted; the local runner and source stayed ignored.

An exploratory Product-boundary projection onto finalized conditional SourceSnapshot Gold found overlap coverage **90/90** intervals, **83/90** contained by one Product card, and **1/90** exact window match. Maximum-overlap card assignment gave source-note exact **82/90**, duration-weighted exact share **.8963**, and duration-weighted note F1 **.9461**. The 90 units are conditional snapshot intervals, **not Harmonic Boundary Gold**; this projection is not an end-to-end acceptance contract.

Passing-chord merge/miss, card-count delta and split/merge correction cost for this local witness are **NOT MEASURABLE WITH THE AVAILABLE BOUNDARY GOLD**. Comparing 25 Product cards to 90 conditional intervals as card-count delta would be invalid. Public synthetic one-beat and half-beat passing-chord controls passed. The private witness was not used for policy selection or tuning; no private raw MIDI, audio, path, filename, note set, interval or fingerprint is tracked.
