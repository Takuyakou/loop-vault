The eight bundled MP3 files are the unmodified Tone.js distribution copies of
the named Salamander Grand Piano samples. They are loaded from the application
bundle at runtime; no CDN or runtime download is used by the Live MIDI piano
monitor. `LICENSE.txt` is the license text from the original repository commit.

Do not replace these files without updating `asset-manifest.json`, its SHA-256
values, and the asset integrity test.
